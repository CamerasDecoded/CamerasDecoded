Autofocus, set up right

How do you set up autofocus on a Sony FX3 — Human and Animal eye detection only, AF-C and Wide for movies, the Tracking limit in movie mode, and AF Assist for taking over focus mid-take?

camerasdecoded.com/camera-select/sony/fx3/autofocus
The answer — share this
Run AF-C with Wide — the cinema default, since movies offer only AF-C and MF — pick the subject the camera actually knows (Human or Animal, nothing else), remember Tracking is not selectable in Movie or S&Q mode, and use AF Assist to grab focus with the lens ring mid-take.
Focus mode (movie): AF-C or MF only  ·  Focus Area: Wide  ·  Recognition: Human / Animal only  ·  Tracking: not in Movie/S&Q  ·  AF Assist: turn the focus ring during movie AF
Cameras Decoded — Camera Select
Step 1

AF-C + Wide

MENU→Focus→AF/MF→Focus Mode

The Focus Mode list runs AF-S (single), AF-A (auto switching), AF-C (continuous), DMF (autofocus, then manual fine-tune) and MF. Movies offer only AF-C and MF. The Focus Area list runs Wide, Zone, Center Fix, Spot S/M/L, Expand Spot and Tracking. The working default for a camera that films people is AF-C with Wide — the whole frame is the search area, and recognition decides what inside it matters. Pre-AF keeps the camera focusing with no half-press (faster response, more battery drain). AF sensitivity runs −6 EV to +20 EV.

Step 2

Human or Animal — nothing else

MENU→Focus→Face/Eye AF→Face/Eye Subject

Face/Eye Prior. in AF is the on-switch: with it On, the camera hunts faces and eyes inside or around the focus area first, and focuses on the eyes automatically. It is locked On in Intelligent Auto. Face/Eye Subject picks the target — Human or Animal, and that is the whole list. For animals, arrange the composition so both eyes and the nose are within the angle of view, and know that the eyes of some types of animals cannot be detected at all. Setting Animal also disables Face Priority in Multi Metering and Regist. Faces Priority. There is no bird mode, no insect mode, no vehicle mode on this body.

Step 3

AF Assist — the focus-ring takeover

MENU→Focus→AF/MF→AF Assist

While shooting a movie with autofocus on, turn the focus ring of the lens and the camera hands you manual focus on the spot — rack to the subject you want. When you stop turning the ring, the subject now in focus becomes the autofocus target, and AF holds it from there. No menu dive, no mode change, mid-take. And Tracking — the Focus Area that locks onto a subject and follows it — is AF-C only, and the manual is specific: Tracking is not selectable in Movie or S&Q mode. In movies, Wide plus subject recognition is the tracking system. Assign Tracking On to a custom key for a temporary lock on something that is not a recognized subject. AF Transition Speed (1 Slow to 7 Fast) is movie-only and sets the tempo of every focus move in your footage.

Step 4

Faster area changes

MENU→Focus→Focus Area→Focus Area Limit

Focus Area Limit lets you check-mark only the areas you actually use, and the unchecked ones leave the menu entirely. Assign Switch Focus Area to a custom key and each press cycles to the next checked area. Switch V/H AF Area remembers a different focus area and frame position for horizontal and vertical shooting — AF Point Only switches the frame position, AF Point + AF Area switches both. It is off by default, and it does not apply during movie shooting — but for the stills you shoot between takes, it means the vertical portrait frame is already where your eye expects it.

Try it now

Set AF-C and Wide with Human recognition, flip to Movie and confirm Tracking is gone from the list, then turn AF Assist on and take focus over with the lens ring mid-take. Finish by opening Focus Area Limit and checking only the areas you use.
