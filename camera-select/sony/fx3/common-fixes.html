Common fixes

Why does your Sony FX3 refuse to record XAVC S-I 4K, why does the log image look flat on the monitor, why has Tracking vanished, and why does the XLR handle record nothing? The four fixes, from the manual.

camerasdecoded.com/camera-select/sony/fx3/common-fixes
The answer — share this
The four classics, all in the manual: a codec that refuses to record is a card too slow for it — S-I 4K needs CFexpress Type A VPG200+ or SDXC V90; a log image that looks flat on the monitor is Gamma Display Assist locked Off while Log Shooting is on — use Display LUT instead; Tracking gone in Movie mode is by design; and an XLR handle that records nothing is the HANDLE AUDIO switch sitting at OFF.
S-I 4K: CFexpress Type A VPG200+ or SDXC V90  ·  Log looks flat: Display LUT, not Gamma Assist  ·  Tracking: not in Movie/S&Q  ·  XLR silent: HANDLE AUDIO switch ON
Cameras Decoded — Camera Select
The codec will not recordThe card is too slow for it. XAVC S-I 4K at 600 Mbps needs a CFexpress Type A card rated VPG200 or higher, or an SDXC V90 card. A V60 or V30 card cannot sustain the write speed, and the camera refuses the take. Slow-motion S-I 4K at 1200 Mbps needs CFexpress Type A VPG200+ only — no SD card qualifies. If the take stops on its own with a temperature warning instead, the cause is heat: Auto Power OFF Temp. on High buys HD about 120 minutes and 4K about 90 minutes continuous at 25 °C, on a tripod.
The log image looks flat on the monitorGamma Display Assist is locked Off while Log Shooting is on. Gamma Display Assist is locked to Off when Log Shooting is set to anything other than Off — it is the wrong tool for log. Use Display LUT (Setup → Display Option → Display LUT) instead: the monitor preview built for log shooting, with Select LUT offering S-Log3, s709, 709(800%) and sixteen user LUTs. The monitor shows the graded image while the recording stays flat S-Log3. Assign Display LUT Switch to a custom key — C4 is free by default.
Tracking has vanished from the menuTracking is not selectable in Movie or S&Q mode. The manual states it directly. In movies, Wide plus subject recognition is the tracking system. Assign Tracking On to a custom key for a temporary lock on something that is not a recognized subject: hold it and the camera tracks what is under the focus area until you let go. And remember what the recognition list holds on this body: Human and Animal only — no bird mode, no vehicle mode.
The XLR handle records nothingThe HANDLE AUDIO switch is OFF. With it OFF, the built-in microphone is live and the handle is ignored entirely. Flip it ON, then match the input chain: the INPUT1/INPUT2 switch must match the microphone (LINE for a mixer, MIC for a dynamic mic or one with its own battery, MIC+48V for a phantom-powered condenser), the INPUT SELECT switch picks which jack feeds each channel, and the AUTO/MAN switch plus AUDIO LEVEL dial set the level while watching the meter. The handle records four channels; 4ch Audio Monitoring picks which pair the headphones hear.
Try it now

Check the card rating against the codec, turn Log Shooting on and switch Display LUT on, confirm Tracking is gone in Movie mode, and flip the HANDLE AUDIO switch on with a mic attached. Finish by confirming the firmware is 7.02.
