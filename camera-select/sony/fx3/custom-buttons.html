Custom button map

Which functions belong on the Sony FX3's buttons — six custom buttons, a movie map that ships with cinema defaults, a C4 that ships as Not set, and separate maps for stills, movies and playback?

camerasdecoded.com/camera-select/sony/fx3/custom-buttons
The answer — share this
Open MENU → Setup → Operation Customize → Custom Key/Dial Set. and build the map around this body's own roles: the movie map already ships with the cinema defaults — C1, C3 and Down toggle aperture, ISO and shutter between auto and manual — C4 ships as Not set and is the free button, and the stills map follows the movie map until you switch Different Set for Still/Mv on.
Path: MENU → Setup → Operation Customize → Custom Key/Dial Set.  ·  C1: L.PressAv Auto/Manual  ·  C3: L.PressISO Auto/Manual  ·  C4: Not set (free)  ·  Left: Zebra Display Select  ·  Right: Peaking Display Select  ·  Maps: separate stills / movie / playback
Cameras Decoded — Camera Select
Step 1

Custom Key/Dial Set.

MENU→Setup→Operation Customize→Custom Key/Dial Set.

Custom Key/Dial Set. holds the assignment list — the movie map and the stills map are separate lists in the same place, and Custom Key Setting holds the playback map. The shipped movie defaults are worth learning first. The dials: Front dial Aperture, Rear dial ISO/EI, Control Wheel Shutter Speed. The toggles: C1 is L.PressAv Auto/Manual (aperture auto/manual), C3 is L.PressISO Auto/Manual (ISO auto/manual), Down is L.PressTv Auto/Manual (shutter auto/manual) — the three switches of Flexible Exposure mode. C2 is White Balance, C5 is Focus Magnifier, C6 is Movie Shooting, and the MOVIE button is Movie Shooting too. The wheel: Left is Zebra Display Select, Right is Peaking Display Select, Center is Focus Standard.

Step 2

Count the surface

MENU→Setup→Operation Customize→Custom Key/Dial Set.

The assignable surface: Custom Buttons 1–6, the Front dial, the Rear dial, the Multi-Slc Center button, the Fn button, the Center button, the Control Wheel (Left, Right, Down), the MOVIE button — and the lens Focus Hold button. Every control carries three separate maps — stills, movie and playback. The stills map defaults to "Follow Custom" — it follows the movie map until you switch Different Set for Still/Mv on. Two more dial options live here: "Separate M mode and other modes" lets the dials behave differently in Manual, and "Separate Flexible Exp. and Intelligent Auto" splits the movie dials between Flexible Exposure and Intelligent Auto.

Step 3

The working map

MENU→Setup→Operation Customize→Custom Key/Dial Set.

Keep the dials and the three auto/manual toggles — C1, C3 and Down are the Flexible Exposure switches, and the dials already run Aperture, ISO/EI and Shutter Speed. Keep Left as Zebra Display Select and Right as Peaking Display Select — exposure and focus checks, one press each. Then spend C4, the free button, on the tool you reach for most: Display LUT Switch if you shoot log, Tracking On if you shoot subjects the camera does not recognize, or Gamma Disp. Assist Sel. if you shoot HLG. And the power move most owners miss: Reg. Custom Shoot Set registers a whole shooting setup — exposure, focus, drive — to Recall Custom hold 1–3, recalled while you hold the assigned key.

Step 4

The overflow

MENU→Setup→Operation Customize→Fn Menu Settings

Six custom buttons still cannot carry a whole cinema camera, so the overflow matters. The Fn menu holds your quick settings — open it with the Fn button — and Touch Func. in Shooting puts a function on a swipe of the touch screen. My Menu is the second overflow: add your most-used items (My Menu → My Menu Setting → Add Item) and they sit one press away at the top of the menu system — Format, Log Shooting Setting, and Auto Power OFF Temp. are the three worth adding first. Then save the entire control scheme with Camera Set. Memory — buttons included — so the map survives a reset or a borrowed body.

Try it now

Open Custom Key/Dial Set., confirm the defaults, assign C4 to the tool you reach for most, add Format and Log Shooting Setting to My Menu, then press every assigned button once. Finish by saving the whole control scheme with Camera Set. Memory.
