Video settings, in depth

What are the right video settings on the a6700 — formats, profiles, and 4K 120p?

camerasdecoded.com/camera-select/sony/a6700/video-settings
The answer — share this
XAVC S 4K as the default, S-Cinetone (PP11) when it ships as-is, Log Shooting (Flexible ISO) when it will be graded — and 120p when you want the slow motion, knowing the frame tightens to 1.6×.
File Format: XAVC S 4K  ·  Movie Settings: 4K 60p 10-bit  ·  Color: PP11 S-Cinetone or Log Shooting On (Flexible ISO)  ·  Auto Power OFF Temp: High
Cameras Decoded — Camera Select
Decision 1

Choose the format by what happens after

MENU→Shooting→Image Quality/Rec→File Format

XAVC HS 4K — smallest files, hardest on the editing computer. XAVC S 4K — the balanced default: 10-bit 4:2:2, edits cleanly, card-friendly. XAVC S-I 4K — every frame stored whole; the biggest files and the easiest edit. Default to XAVC S 4K and change only with a reason.

Decision 2

Log lives in its own menu here

MENU→Shooting→Image Quality/Rec→Log Shooting Setting

Unlike the a7 IV, the a6700 has no S-Log3 profile preset — no PP8. Log shooting is a switch: Log Shooting → On (Flexible ISO), which applies S-Log3 with a LUT workflow; there is no Cine EI mode on this body. While Log is on, Picture Profile is fixed to Off. For footage that ships as-is, use PP11 — S-Cinetone.

Decision 3

4K 120p: slow motion, tighter frame

MENU→Shooting→Image Quality/Rec→Movie Settings

4K 120p is a regular Rec Frame Rate in Movie Settings — no S&Q detour. The trade: at 120p the angle of view tightens to ≈1.6× the lens marking (stills and other movie modes run ≈1.5×), so a 24mm frames like roughly a 38mm. Focus breathing compensation is also unavailable at 120p.

Decision 4

Buy recording time in Setup

MENU→Setup→Power Setting Option→Auto Power OFF Temp.

Set Auto Power OFF Temp. → High and the camera tolerates more heat before protecting itself: Sony's guide rates roughly 30 minutes of continuous 4K at 25°C on High. Set it on setup day, not during the shoot.
