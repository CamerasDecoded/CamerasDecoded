Autofocus, set up right

What are the right autofocus settings on the Sony a6400 — Tracking, Eye AF, and animal detection?

camerasdecoded.com/camera-select/sony/a6400/autofocus
The answer — share this
Continuous AF plus the Tracking focus area for anything that moves; Face/Eye Priority on with Subject Detection matched to the subject — Human, or Animal for animal eyes only. There is no bird category on this body, and Tracking steps aside when Animal detection is on.
Focus Mode: Camera Settings1 → Focus Mode → Continuous AF  ·  Area: Camera Settings1 → Focus Area → Tracking  ·  Detection: Camera Settings1 → Face/Eye AF Set. → Subject Detection  ·  Touch: Setup → Touch Operation → On
Cameras Decoded — Camera Select
Decision 1

Tracking, with its one condition

MENU→Camera Settings1→Focus Area

Set Focus Mode to Continuous AF, then Focus Area to Tracking. Half-press and the camera marks the subject and follows it across the frame; the start area (Wide, Zone, Flexible Spot) is chosen on the Tracking screen. Sony’s guide states the condition plainly: Tracking is available only in Continuous AF, and only for stills — the Tracking area cannot be selected while shooting movies.

Decision 2

Whose eyes?

MENU→Camera Settings1→Face/Eye AF Set.

Set Face/Eye Priority in AF to On, then Subject Detection to match the subject: Human, or Animal. For animals the guide is exact — it detects animal eyes; animal faces are not detected — and Right/Left Eye Select cannot be used with Animal. The menu is documented for firmware Ver.2.00 and later; Animal detection arrived with that update, so check Setup → Version on a second-hand body.

Decision 3

The honest limits

MENU→Camera Settings2→Func. of Touch Operation

There is no bird category on this body: the detection list is Human and Animal, nothing else. And with Subject Detection set to Animal, the Tracking focus area cannot be selected — for animals, work in Wide or Zone with Eye AF. Touch Tracking is the addition that works everywhere: Touch Operation On in Setup, Func. of Touch Operation set to Touch Tracking, then tap any subject on the screen to follow it, in stills and movies.

Try it now

Track a person with AF-C + Tracking, flip Subject Detection to Animal for a pet, then tap-to-track with Touch Tracking — three drills, one sitting.
