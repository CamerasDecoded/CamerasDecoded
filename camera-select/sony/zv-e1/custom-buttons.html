Custom button map

Which functions belong on the Sony ZV-E1's buttons — only two custom buttons, a Delete button that ships as Product Showcase, a C1 that ships as Background Defocus, and separate maps for stills, movies and playback?

camerasdecoded.com/camera-select/sony/zv-e1/custom-buttons
The answer — share this
Open MENU → Setup → Operation Customize → Custom Key/Dial Set. and build the map around this body's own roles: keep C1 as Background Defocus and the Delete button as Product Showcase Set, put Tracking On and SteadyShot Select where your thumb lives, and use the Fn menu and My Menu as the overflow — because with only C1 and C2, every assignment counts.
Path: MENU → Setup → Operation Customize → Custom Key/Dial Set.  ·  C1: Background Defocus  ·  Delete: Product Showcase Set  ·  C2: Step Zoom  ·  Maps: separate stills / movie / playback

Step 1

Custom Key/Dial Set.

MENU→Setup→Operation Customize→Custom Key/Dial Set.

Custom Key/Dial Set. holds the assignment list. The shipped defaults are worth learning first. C1 is Background Defocus, C2 is Step Zoom, the Delete button is Product Showcase Set. The center of the control wheel is Focus Standard, Left is Self-timer, Right is ISO, Down is Exposure Comp. The Control Dial and Control Wheel both run Av/Tv (Av in M on the dial, Tv in M on the wheel), and the lens Focus Hold button is Focus Hold. The Fn button opens the Fn menu; a swipe up on the touch screen opens it too.

Step 2

Count the surface

MENU→Setup→Operation Customize→Custom Key/Dial Set.

The assignable surface is small on purpose: the MOVIE button, Custom Button 1, the Control Dial, Custom Button 2, the Fn button, the Control Wheel (Center, Left, Right, Down), the Delete button — and the lens Focus Hold button. Only C1 and C2 exist; there is no C3 or C4. Every control carries three separate maps — stills, movie and playback — so the same button can do one thing while shooting stills and another while recording. The movie map mostly follows the stills map ("Follow Custom") except where it differs — Left becomes Drive Mode in movies. Playback has its own assignments (C2 becomes Magnification, Fn becomes Send). A "Separate M mode and other modes" option lets the dials behave differently in Manual.

Step 3

The working map

MENU→Setup→Operation Customize→Custom Key/Dial Set.

Keep C1 as Background Defocus — the one-press bokeh switch is the reason the button exists — and keep the Delete button as Product Showcase Set. Then spend the wheel: Tracking On gives a temporary tracking lock on anything, which matters because Tracking is not selectable as a Focus Area in Movie mode. SteadyShot Select toggles stabilization on and off without a menu dive — the tripod switch. Display LUT Switch toggles the monitor LUT preview while shooting log. Anti-flicker Tv Scan hunts the flicker-free shutter speed under LED lighting. The fine print: Product Showcase On locks Subject Recog in AF to Off and sets Focus Area to Zone, and Background Defocus locks the aperture while it is on.

Step 4

Fn menu and My Menu

MENU→Setup→Operation Customize→Fn Menu Settings

The Fn menu holds twelve quick settings — open it with the Fn button or a swipe up on the touch screen — and Touch Func. in Shooting defaults to Touch Tracking, so tapping a subject on the screen tracks it. My Menu is the second overflow: add your most-used items (My Menu → My Menu Setting → Add Item) and they sit one press away at the top of the menu system — Format, Log Shooting Setting, and Auto Power OFF Temp. are the three worth adding first. When the map is built, save it with Camera Set. Memory so the control scheme — buttons included — is recalled with the rest of the setup and survives a settings reset.

Try it now

Build the map now — confirm the vlog duo, assign Tracking On and SteadyShot Select, add Format and Log Shooting Setting to My Menu — press every assigned button once to prove it, then save the whole scheme with Camera Set. Memory.
