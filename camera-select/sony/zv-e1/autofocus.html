Autofocus, set up right

How do you set up autofocus on a Sony ZV-E1 — seven subject recognition types, AF-C and Wide for vlogging, the Tracking limit in movie mode, and AF Assist for taking over focus mid-take?

camerasdecoded.com/camera-select/sony/zv-e1/autofocus
The answer — share this
Run AF-C with Wide — the vlog default, since movies offer only AF-C and MF — pick the subject the camera actually knows (Human, Animal/Bird, Animal, Bird, Insect, Car/Train, Airplane), remember Tracking is not selectable in Movie or S&Q mode, and use AF Assist to grab focus with the lens ring mid-take.
Focus mode (movie): AF-C or MF only  ·  Focus Area: Wide  ·  Recognition: 7 types  ·  Tracking: not in Movie/S&Q  ·  AF Assist: turn the focus ring during movie AF
Cameras Decoded — Camera Select
Step 1

AF-C + Wide

MENU→Focus→AF/MF→Focus Mode

The Focus Mode list runs AF-S (single), AF-A (auto switching — only with phase-detection-capable lenses), AF-C (continuous), DMF (autofocus, then manual fine-tune) and MF. Movies offer only AF-C and MF. The Focus Area list runs Wide, Zone, Center Fix, Spot S/M/L, Expand Spot and Tracking. The working default for a camera that films people is AF-C with Wide — the whole frame is the search area, and recognition decides what inside it matters. Pre-AF keeps the camera focusing with no half-press (faster response, more battery drain, and the screen may shake while it hunts). AF sensitivity runs −6 EV to +20 EV.

Step 2

The movie-mode limit

MENU→Focus→Focus Area→Focus Area

Tracking — the Focus Area that locks onto a subject and follows it around the frame — is AF-C only, and the manual is specific: Tracking is not selectable in Movie or S&Q mode. Flip the Still/Movie/S&Q switch to Movie and Tracking leaves the list entirely. In movies, Wide plus subject recognition is the tracking system — the camera finds the recognized subject anywhere in the frame and holds it. Assign Tracking On to a custom key for a temporary lock on something that is not a recognized subject: hold it and the camera tracks what is under the focus area until you let go. AF Transition Speed (1 Slow to 7 Fast) is movie-only and sets the tempo of every focus move in your footage.

Know this: Cinematic Vlog Set overrides AF Transition Speed with its own HI/MID/LO while active, and the menu setting is temporarily invalid — the Video lesson covers it.
Step 3

AF Assist — the focus-ring takeover

MENU→Focus→AF/MF→AF Assist

While shooting a movie with autofocus on, turn the focus ring of the lens and the camera hands you manual focus on the spot — rack to the subject you want. When you stop turning the ring, the subject now in focus becomes the autofocus target, and AF holds it from there. No menu dive, no mode change, mid-take. Pair it with Focus Map (Focus → Focus Assistant): during a movie it paints the frame with dots — cool-colored dots behind the in-focus region, warm-colored dots in front — so you can see which way focus needs to travel. The dots are never recorded. Focus Map is unavailable with the Focus Magnifier, digital zoom, streaming, non-phase-detection lenses, or a mount adaptor.

Step 4

Tune the recognition

MENU→Focus→Subject Recognition→Subject Recog in AF

Subject Recog in AF offers seven targets: Human, Animal/Bird, Animal, Bird, Insect, Car/Train, Airplane. The detailed settings decide the temperament: Tracking Shift Range (1–5) sets how far the focus area may wander from the subject; Tracking Persistence Lvl (1–5) sets how long the camera holds a subject that disappears behind something; Recognition Sensitivity (1–5) sets how eagerly the camera locks onto a recognized subject; Recognition Priority chooses between Animal and Bird when both are in frame under Animal/Bird; Recognition Part chooses Eye/Head/Body, Eye/Head, or Eye, with Right/Left eye priority inside the eye setting. For a vlog, the honest defaults work — Human recognition, Eye/Head/Body, middle values. Change them when a specific shoot misbehaves, not before.

Try it now

Set AF-C and Wide with Human recognition, flip to Movie and confirm Tracking is gone from the list, then turn AF Assist on and take focus over with the lens ring mid-take. Finish by turning Focus Map on and reading the dots.
