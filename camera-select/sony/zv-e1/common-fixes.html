Common fixes

Why does your Sony ZV-E1 clip the flash, why is 4K missing from the menu, why is there no 120p, and why does it stop recording? The four fixes, from the manual.

camerasdecoded.com/camera-select/sony/zv-e1/common-fixes
The answer — share this
The four classics, all in the manual: a flash that clips is the electronic shutter — sync is 1/30, no faster; 4K missing from the menu means APS-C/Super 35mm Shooting is On; 120p missing means the free 4K 120p upgrade license is not installed; and a take that stops is heat (Auto Power OFF Temp.) or a card too slow for the codec.
Flash sync: 1/30 (electronic shutter only)  ·  4K missing: APS-C/Super 35mm Shooting Off  ·  120p missing: install the free license  ·  Take stops: Auto Power OFF Temp. High + V90 card for S-I

The flash clips — half the frame is darkThe shutter is faster than the 1/30 sync speed. The ZV-E1 has no mechanical shutter, so flash sync is 1/30s full-frame (1/60 in APS-C mode). Faster than that and the shutter outruns the flash. Keep the shutter at 1/30 or slower with flash. Silent Mode disables flash shooting outright — if the flash will not fire at all, check Silent Mode first. The anti-flicker Var. Shutter cannot be used with flash (except with Flash Off).
4K is missing from the menuAPS-C/Super 35mm Shooting is On. With it on, the camera crops to the APS-C area of the sensor and 4K recording is disabled entirely — the 4K options simply leave the menu. Turn it Off and they return. It lives in the Image Quality group and persists across power cycles, so it is easy to switch on by accident. If 4K is missing and APS-C is already off, check the card: a card slower than the codec needs will refuse the take, though it does not hide the menu item.
There is no 120p anywhereThe free 4K 120p upgrade license is not installed. 4K 120p requires Sony's free 4K 120p upgrade license, installed once from Creators' Cloud. Until it is installed, the 120p frame rates do not exist in the menu. The limits that come with it: no proxy recording, no Smart/Clear/Digital zoom, and no Active or Dynamic Active SteadyShot at 120p, plus a V60 card or better for the 280 Mbps rate. The license also unlocks 240fps in HD slow motion. If 120p is installed and still missing, check the file format: 120p is not available with XAVC S-I.
The take stops on its ownHeat, or a card too slow. Auto Power OFF Temp. on Standard stops recording sooner; set it to High for longer takes — HD about 90 minutes, 4K about 30 minutes continuous at 25 °C — and use a tripod, since holding the camera on High risks low-temperature burns. At 40 °C both figures drop to about 20 minutes. The card: XAVC S-I 4K at 600 Mbps needs SDXC V90 or better, 4K 120p needs V60 or better. And two by-design facts: S&Q footage never has sound, and proxy recording is impossible at 120p/100p and with XAVC S-I.
Try it now

Set the shutter to 1/30 and fire the flash once, confirm APS-C is off, check whether 120p is in Movie Settings, and confirm the firmware is 2.02.
