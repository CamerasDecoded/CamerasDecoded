Video, set up right

How do you set up video on a Sony ZV-E1 — 4K up to 60p out of the box, 120p behind a free license, S-Log3 without the picture profiles other Sonys have, and Cinematic Vlog with black bars recorded in?

camerasdecoded.com/camera-select/sony/zv-e1/video-settings
The answer — share this
Choose the codec for the job — XAVC HS 4K for small files, XAVC S 4K for compatibility, XAVC S-I 4K for editing (V90 card required) — know that 4K 120p needs the free upgrade license, reach S-Log3 through Log Shooting Setting (there are no PP7/PP8/PP9 presets here), shoot it at ISO 640 or 12,800, and let Cinematic Vlog Set do the look when the edit needs to be fast.
Formats: XAVC HS / S / S-I 4K  ·  Top mode: 4K/60p (120p with license)  ·  S-Log3: Log Shooting Setting → On (Flexible ISO)  ·  Dual native ISO: 640 / 12,800  ·  Card: S-I 4K needs V90
Cameras Decoded — Camera Select
Step 1

The codec ladder

MENU→Shooting→Image Quality/Rec→File Format

File Format offers four codecs. XAVC HS 4K (H.265) is the small-file pick — efficient compression, harder to edit. XAVC S 4K (H.264) is the compatible pick — every editor opens it. XAVC S HD covers Full HD. XAVC S-I 4K/HD (Intra) is the editing pick — every frame stored whole, at 600 Mbps in 4K at 60p (500 Mbps at the lower rate), 300/250 Mbps at 30p, 240 Mbps at 24p. Out of the box the top mode is 4K/60p in 4:2:2 10-bit; Full HD runs to 120p. Turning APS-C/Super 35mm Shooting On disables 4K recording entirely — the 4K options simply leave the menu. S&Q (slow and quick motion) records no sound, ever; frame rates run to 120fps, and 240fps in HD with the 4K 120p license.

Step 2

The 120p license

MENU→Shooting→Image Quality/Rec→Movie Settings

The ZV-E1 records 4K at 120p — with a full-frame angle of view, no crop — but the frame rates do not appear in the menu until you install Sony's free 4K 120p upgrade license from Creators' Cloud. Install it once and the rates unlock: XAVC HS 4K 120p at 280M 4:2:2 10-bit or 200M 4:2:0 10-bit; XAVC S 4K 120p at 280M 4:2:2 10-bit or 200M 4:2:0 8-bit. 120p is not available with XAVC S-I. At 120p there is no proxy recording, no Smart/Clear/Digital zoom, and no Active or Dynamic Active SteadyShot. 4K 120p at 280 Mbps needs a V60 card or better; the license also unlocks 240fps in HD slow motion (XAVC S HD only).

Know this: the card table is the whole story for the single slot. XAVC S-I 4K at 600 Mbps needs SDXC V90 or better; S-I HD at 222 Mbps needs V90 too. 4K up to 200 Mbps needs U3 or better. Proxy recording needs V60 at 4K 200 Mbps — and proxy is impossible at 120p/100p and with XAVC S-I.
Step 3

Log, the ZV-E1 way

MENU→Shooting→Image Quality/Rec→Log Shooting Setting

On most Sonys, S-Log3 lives in Picture Profile PP8 or PP9. Not here. The ZV-E1's Picture Profiles run PP1–PP6, PP10 and PP11 (PP10 is HLG2 HDR, PP11 is S-Cinetone) plus four LUT slots — there are no PP7, PP8 or PP9 presets at all. S-Log3 is reached only through Log Shooting Setting: Off, or On (Flexible ISO). There is no Cine EI and no Cine EI Quick on this body — Flexible ISO is the only log mode. The color gamut offers S-Gamut3.Cine/S-Log3 or S-Gamut3/S-Log3. Flexible ISO starts at ISO 640 — the S-Log3 floor — and the sensor's second native ISO is 12,800, where the image cleans up again in the dark. Display LUT previews the grade on the monitor (Setup → Display Option) while the file stays flat, and Select LUT offers S-Log3, s709 and 709(800%) presets plus sixteen user LUTs. Embed LUT File is locked Off on SD and SDHC cards.

Step 4

Cinematic Vlog and framing

MENU→Shooting→Shooting Option→Cinematic Vlog Set

Cinematic Vlog Set is the fast-edit path: turn it on and the camera records black bars top and bottom as part of the image (the recorded aspect is still 16:9 — the visible area is cinemascope), locks the frame rate to 24fps, and offers a Look (S-Cinetone, CLEAN, CHIC, FRESH, MONO, or User1–16 LUTs from firmware 2.00) and a Mood (AUTO, GOLD, OCEAN, FOREST — not with MONO). White balance locks to Auto while it is on, and the set is not reflected when you press MOVIE in stills mode, nor with XAVC S-I 4K in S&Q. Auto Framing (movie only, not S&Q) crops from 4K to follow you around the frame — Start When Tracking, Auto Start, or Auto Start with a 15s/30s switch; Crop Level Large/Medium/Small. Framing Stabilizer locks the subject at a chosen position (Center, or Manual via touch or the center button). Both crop from 4K, so Sony recommends an HD file format to avoid quality loss; the monitor always shows the full angle with a crop frame overlay.

Step 5

Steady, live, heard

MENU→Network→Streaming→USB Streaming

SteadyShot (movie) runs Dynamic Active, Active, Standard, Off — strongest first. Active narrows the angle of view; Dynamic Active crops further and disables Clear Image Zoom; both are unavailable at 120p/100p or S&Q 120fps and up. Turn stabilization off on a tripod. USB Streaming turns the camera into a webcam — up to 4K 30p, HD 60p or 720p 30p, no capture card — but it needs a SuperSpeed USB 5Gbps cable for 4K/HD (a USB 2.0 cable drops output to 720p), and you can record to the card while streaming. Network Streaming (firmware 2.00+) streams direct to a server from the Creators' App — server and stream key set on the phone, start/stop on the center button. Audio starts with the built-in 3-capsule mic (directivity Auto / Front / All Directions / Rear; wind reduction built-in-mic only) and tops out at 48kHz/24-bit 4-channel digital audio through the Multi Interface Shoe with a compatible mic. 32-bit float audio is not available on this body. Heat: Auto Power OFF Temp. on High buys HD ~90 min / 4K ~30 min continuous at 25 °C — on a tripod, since holding the camera risks low-temperature burns.

Try it now

Set XAVC S 4K at 24p, turn Log Shooting Setting on at ISO 640 with the Display LUT preview, then turn Cinematic Vlog Set on and watch the black bars appear. Finish by checking whether 120p is in Movie Settings — if not, install the free license.
