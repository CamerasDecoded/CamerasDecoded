Common fixes

Why won't your Fujifilm GFX 100 II fire the flash, why does the battery drain so fast, why won't it detect a face, and why won't it take a picture? The four fixes, from the manual.

camerasdecoded.com/camera-select/fujifilm/gfx100ii/common-fixes
The answer — share this
The four classics, all in the manual: a flash that won't fire is the electronic shutter — choose a shutter type that does not use it; a battery that drains fast is usually PRE-AF left on; a face or subject that won't be detected is obscured, too small, turned away, or the wrong detection is live; and a shutter that takes no picture is a full card, an unformatted card, a frame number at 999-9999, or an adapted lens without SHOOT WITHOUT LENS.
Flash: SHOOTING SETTING → SHUTTER TYPE → not electronic  ·  Battery: AF/MF SETTING → PRE-AF OFF  ·  Detection: face/subject checklist  ·  No picture: card → format → frame number → SHOOT WITHOUT LENS
Cameras Decoded — Camera Select
The flash won't fireThe electronic shutter is selected. The manual states it plainly: the flash will not fire when the electronic shutter is used. Choose a SHUTTER TYPE that does not use the electronic shutter — MECHANICAL SHUTTER or E-FRONT CURTAIN SHUTTER. The troubleshooting list adds: the flash is disabled in its own settings, the battery is exhausted, or the camera is in bracketing or continuous mode. If the flash fires but does not fully light the subject, check the shutter speed against the 1/125 sync speed.
The battery runs down quicklyPRE-AF is focusing while you wait. With PRE-AF ON, the camera keeps adjusting focus even when the shutter button is not pressed halfway — the manual lists it under a battery that runs down quickly. Turn it OFF when instant response is not the job. The same list names a cold battery (warm it in a pocket, reinsert just before shooting), dirt on the terminals (soft, dry cloth), and an old battery (check BATTERY AGE under USER SETTING). PERFORMANCE on BOOST also drains faster than NORMAL by design.
No face — or no subject — is detectedWork the checklist, starting with which detection is live. Face and subject detection are mutually exclusive: enabling one switches the other off. Then the manual's checklist. No face: obscured by sunglasses, a hat or long hair; occupying only a small area of the frame; turned away; FACE/EYE DETECTION SETTING off; camera tilted; face poorly lit. No subject: partially hidden; occupying only a small portion of the frame — get closer; SUBJECT DETECTION off; poorly lit.
No picture is taken when the shutter button is pressedThe card, the counter, or the adapter. The manual's list, in order: the memory card is full; the card is not formatted (format it in the camera); dirt on the card contacts; the card is damaged; the battery is exhausted; the camera turned off automatically; or you are using a third-party mount adapter — select ON for SHOOT WITHOUT LENS. One more silent stop: if FRAME NO. reaches 999-9999, the shutter release is disabled until you insert a formatted card.
Try it now

Set the shutter to mechanical and fire the flash once, confirm PRE-AF is off, check which detection is live, and run PIXEL MAPPING if you have ever seen bright spots.
