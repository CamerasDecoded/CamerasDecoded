Autofocus, set up right

How do you set up autofocus and subject detection on a Fujifilm GFX 100 II — the hybrid AF system, its six detection types, and the trap where face and subject detection switch each other off?

camerasdecoded.com/camera-select/fujifilm/gfx100ii/autofocus
The answer — share this
Run AF-C with WIDE/TRACKING, choose your detection — face/eye OR subject, never both, because enabling one switches the other off — pick the subject type the camera actually knows (ANIMAL, BIRD, AUTOMOBILE, MOTORCYCLE&BIKE, AIRPLANE, TRAIN), then tune AF-C CUSTOM SETTINGS so the camera holds focus the way your subjects move.
Focus mode: C  ·  AF Mode: WIDE/TRACKING  ·  Face/eye and subject detection are mutually exclusive  ·  Detection: AF/MF SETTING → SUBJECT DETECTION SETTING  ·  Focus points: 117 or 425
Cameras Decoded — Camera Select
Step 1

AF-C + WIDE/TRACKING

MENU→AF/MF SETTING→AF MODE

The focus mode selector picks S (single), C (continuous) or M (manual). The AF modes: SINGLE POINT for pinpoint focus on a selected subject, ZONE for a cluster of points that makes moving subjects easier to follow, WIDE in AF-S / WIDE/TRACKING in AF-C for automatic focus across the frame with tracking, and ALL, which cycles through the modes chosen in AF MODE ALL SETTING (set separately for AF-S and AF-C) with the rear command dial. NUMBER OF FOCUS POINTS offers 117 (9×13) or 425 (17×25). The working default for movers is C with WIDE/TRACKING.

Step 2

Faces — and the trap

MENU→AF/MF SETTING→FACE/EYE DETECTION SETTING

FACE/EYE DETECTION SETTING gives detected faces priority for focus and exposure. Eye detection offers EYE OFF (faces only), EYE AUTO (the camera chooses the eye), RIGHT EYE PRIORITY or LEFT EYE PRIORITY. Tap the display or use the focus stick (with WIDE selected) to pick a different face; assign RIGHT/LEFT EYE SWITCH to a function button to swap eyes mid-shoot. The trap: enabling face detection automatically selects OFF for SUBJECT DETECTION SETTING, and enabling subject detection automatically selects OFF for face/eye detection — the manual states it in both directions. The camera runs one intelligence at a time. Fn1 ships as FACE DETECTION ON/OFF and Fn2 as SUBJECT DETECTION ON/OFF, so the swap is two front-button presses.

Know this: if the camera cannot detect eyes because they are hidden by hair, glasses or other objects, it falls back to focusing on the face. A face that occupies only a small area of the frame, is turned away, or is poorly lit may not be detected at all — the manual's troubleshooting lists all three.
Step 3

The six detection types

MENU→AF/MF SETTING→SUBJECT DETECTION SETTING

SUBJECT DETECTION SETTING offers six types, and the manual names what each covers. ANIMAL: dogs and cats. BIRD: birds and insects. AUTOMOBILE: the body or front ends of cars, primarily motorsport types. MOTORCYCLE&BIKE: the riders of motorcycles and bicycles. AIRPLANE: the cockpits, noses or bodies of airplanes and drones. TRAIN: the driver compartments or front ends of trains. ANIMAL is dogs and cats only — for horses and other wildlife, use ZONE or SINGLE POINT and track manually. Detection fails when the subject is partially hidden, too small in the frame, or poorly lit: get closer, get light, get a clear view.

Step 4

How stubborn: AF-C CUSTOM SETTINGS

MENU→AF/MF SETTING→AF-C CUSTOM SETTINGS

Six sets frame the behavior: SET 1 MULTI PURPOSE (standard, for the typical range of moving subjects), SET 2 IGNORE OBSTACLES & CONTINUE TO TRACK SUBJECT (holds the chosen subject when other objects enter the focus area), SET 3 FOR ACCELERATING/DECELERATING SUBJECT, SET 4 FOR SUDDENLY APPEARING SUBJECT (focuses quickly on subjects entering the focus area, or when rapidly switching subjects), SET 5 FOR ERRATICALLY MOVING & ACCEL./DECEL. SUBJECT, and SET 6 CUSTOM — TRACKING SENSITIVITY (0–4; higher waits longer before switching subjects), SPEED TRACKING SENSITIVITY (0–2) and ZONE AREA SWITCHING, tuned from the values of Sets 1–5. PRE-AF keeps the camera focusing with no half-press (faster response, more battery drain). ZONE CUSTOM SETTING builds three custom focus zones for ZONE mode, and STORE AF MODE BY ORIENTATION remembers the AF mode and focus area per orientation.

Try it now

Set C and WIDE/TRACKING, turn face detection on with EYE AUTO, then turn subject detection on and confirm face detection reads OFF — feel the trap once. Finish by toggling both with Fn1 and Fn2.
