Video, set up right

How do you set up video on a Fujifilm GFX 100 II — 8K/30p and 4K/60p in 10-bit, ProRes in the camera, F-Log2C, and the card rule that decides what your SD slot can record?

camerasdecoded.com/camera-select/fujifilm/gfx100ii/video-settings
The answer — share this
Choose IMAGE FORMAT for your lens — GF for GF glass — then MOVIE MODE up to 8K/30p or 4K/60p in 4:2:2 10-bit, or Apple ProRes 422 for edit-ready files; know the card rule before you roll (ProRes and 720 Mbps need the CFexpress slot), pick F-Log2 or F-Log2C for grading with its ISO 800 floor, and plan heat with the optional cooling fan on long takes.
Format: MOVIE SETTING → IMAGE FORMAT → GF  ·  Top modes: 8K/30p, 4K/60p, FHD/120p  ·  ProRes = CFexpress only  ·  F-Log2/F-Log2C floor: ISO 800  ·  Fan: optional FAN-001
Cameras Decoded — Camera Select
Step 1

IMAGE FORMAT, then MOVIE MODE

MENU→MOVIE SETTING→IMAGE FORMAT

IMAGE FORMAT matches the movie format to the lens: GF for GF Series lenses, Premista for Fujinon Premista cinema zooms, 35mm for 35mm-format lenses, ANAMORPHIC(35mm) with desqueeze display and magnification setting for anamorphic glass. MOVIE MODE then picks the frame size, aspect ratio and frame rate: up to 8K/30p and 4K/60p in 4:2:2 10-bit in-camera, with FHD/120p for slow motion; frame rates 59.94P, 50P, 29.97P, 25P, 24P, 23.98P. MEDIA REC SETTING chooses the file type, bit rate and destination card slot. Movie image-quality settings (film simulation, dynamic range, white balance, tone curve) are adjusted separately from stills.

Step 2

The card rule

MENU→MOVIE SETTING→MEDIA REC SETTING

Codecs: H.265 10-bit MOV 4:2:2/4:2:0 at 720, 360, 200, 100 or 50 Mbps; H.264 8-bit MOV/MP4 4:2:0; ProRes 422 HQ, ProRes 422 and ProRes 422 LT in 10-bit MOV 4:2:2. The manual's movie chapter ties each to a card: record ProRes movies to CFexpress cards; 720 Mbps to CFexpress or a card with Video Speed Class V90 or better; 360 Mbps to CFexpress or V60 or better; 100 or 200 Mbps to CFexpress or a card with UHS Speed Class 3 or better. Proxy recording exists for ProRes (H.264 or ProRes Proxy duplicates) but is not available at 8K with 29.97P or 25P, and deleting the original deletes the proxy.

Know this: the SD slot is a real video slot at the lighter rates — but ProRes and the top bit rate belong to the CFexpress card in Slot 1 alone. Point MEDIA REC SETTING at the card that can hold the quality you picked.
Step 3

Log, three generations

MENU→MOVIE SETTING→F-Log/HLG RECORDING

F-Log/HLG RECORDING chooses the gamma for footage recorded to the card and output over HDMI: P (film simulation), F-Log (ISO 400–12800), F-Log2 (ISO 800–12800, wider dynamic range than F-Log), F-Log2C (ISO 800–12800, an even wider gamut than F-Log and F-Log2 — added in firmware 2.30), and HLG (ISO 640–12800, conforming to ITU-R BT2100). F-Log VIEW ASSIST previews the grade on the monitor while the file stays flat. Movie DYNAMIC RANGE (100/200/400%) is available only when P is selected; there is no AUTO in movie mode — 200% needs ISO 200–12800, 400% needs ISO 400–12800.

Step 4

Slow motion and heat

MENU→MOVIE SETTING→HIGH SPEED REC

HIGH SPEED REC records at 100P or 120P in FHD 16:9 or 17:9, with the playback rate selectable from 23.98P to 59.94P; high-speed movies are recorded with no sound. An HDMI ONLY option records footage only to external recorders. This body has no built-in cooling fan — the FAN-001 is an optional accessory, and COOLING FAN SETTING runs it at AUTO1 (slow, as needed), AUTO2 (high, as needed — the increased fan noise may be audible in movies), LOW, HIGH or OFF. Battery life for movie capture is about 60 minutes of actual 4K recording per charge at 29.97 fps.

Step 5

Out of the camera

MENU→MOVIE SETTING→HDMI OUTPUT SETTING

HDMI RAW output is supported with Atomos and Blackmagic Design external recorders (12-bit Apple ProRes RAW and Blackmagic RAW); HDMI REC CONTROL sends movie start and stop signals to the HDMI device. TIME CODE SYNC. SETTING synchronizes time codes, including with a USB device from firmware 2.30. Audio is LPCM 24-bit/48 kHz, two channels in-camera and four channels via an XLR microphone adapter; AAC for MP4 recordings. 32-bit float audio is not available on this body. APERTURE UNIT FOR CINEMA LENS (firmware 2.50) displays aperture as T-numbers with G-mount cinema lenses; power zoom lenses (GF32-90mmT3.5 PZ OIS WR) are supported from the same update.

Try it now

Set GF format and 4K/60p, pick F-Log2 with the View Assist preview on, then flip IMAGE FORMAT to 35mm and watch the frame change. Finish by pointing MEDIA REC SETTING at the card that can hold your bit rate.
