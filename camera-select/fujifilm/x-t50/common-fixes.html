Common Fixes

What fixes the Fujifilm X-T50's known problems — detection that switches off, a film simulation that won't change, flash that won't fire?

https://camerasdecoded.com/camera-select/fujifilm/x-t50/common-fixes.html
THE ANSWER
Eye AF that vanished means subject detection is on — the two systems switch each other off. A film simulation that won’t change means the dial is on a named position — only C lets the menu choose. A flash that won’t fire is the electronic shutter. And if the phone app won’t connect after firmware 1.30, the old Camera Remote app is done — XApp is the only way in now.
Eye AF: AF/MF SETTING → SUBJECT DETECTION SETTING → OFF · Film sim: turn the dial to C · Flash: SHOOTING SETTING → SHUTTER TYPE → MECHANICAL · App: install FUJIFILM XApp
Free lesson — the guided run walks every step.
STEP 1

One system turns the other off

MENU→AF/MF SETTING→SUBJECT DETECTION SETTING

Eye AF finds no faces because subject detection is on — enabling it automatically switches FACE/EYE DETECTION to OFF, and enabling face detection switches subject detection OFF. Turn subject detection OFF and eye AF comes back immediately. If you shoot people and animals in the same session, put both toggles on buttons — Fn1 already toggles face detection.

STEP 2

The dial wins

MENU→IMAGE QUALITY SETTING→FILM SIMULATION

The film simulation will not change from the menu, the Q menu, a function button, or a saved custom setting — because the film simulation dial is on a named position. Those choices only apply when the dial is on C. Turn the dial to C first, then the menu choice takes hold. FS1–FS3 hold your own assignments, with separate stills and movie settings.

STEP 3

Blame the electronic shutter

MENU→SHOOTING SETTING→SHUTTER TYPE

The pop-up flash will not fire when the electronic shutter is used — set SHUTTER TYPE to MECHANICAL SHUTTER (MECHANICAL + ELECTRONIC can still choose the electronic shutter). It also will not fire in panorama mode, or with SOUND & FLASH off. In S or M mode, keep the shutter speed slower than 1/180 s. And the flash is small — GN 3.9 at ISO 100 — so keep subjects close.

STEP 4

The app that stopped connecting

MENU→SET UP→USER SETTING→FIRMWARE UPDATE

Firmware 1.30 changed the wireless system for security: the old Camera Remote app no longer connects, the WIRELESS COMMUNICATION menu item is gone, and there is no rollback. Install FUJIFILM XApp and pair again. Current firmware is Ver. 1.32 — check yours at USER SETTING > FIRMWARE UPDATE. And if the shutter will not fire at all, check RELEASE/FOCUS PRIORITY: on FOCUS, the camera refuses to shoot until focus locks.

TRY IT NOW

Turn subject detection off and watch eye AF return; turn the film simulation dial to C and change the palette from the menu; switch SHUTTER TYPE to MECHANICAL and test the pop-up flash.
