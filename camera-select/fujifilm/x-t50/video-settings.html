Video Settings

What are the best video settings on the Fujifilm X-T50 — 6.2K, 4K, log, slow motion?

https://camerasdecoded.com/camera-select/fujifilm/x-t50/video-settings.html
THE ANSWER
6.2K and 4K HQ come from a 1.23x crop — this is not open gate — so pick 4K sub-sampled for the full width, shoot H.265 10-bit at 200 Mbps on a V60 card, log at F-Log2 from ISO 1000, and remember high-speed footage is always silent.
Mode: MOVIE SETTING → MOVIE MODE · Codec: MOVIE SETTING → MEDIA RECORD SETTING · Log: MOVIE SETTING → F-Log/HLG RECORDING · Heat: SET UP → POWER MANAGEMENT → AUTO POWER OFF TEMP.
Pro lesson — the deep steps are in the guided run.
STEP 1

6.2K is cropped, not open gate

MENU→MOVIE SETTING→MOVIE MODE

The 6.2K and 4K HQ modes read a 1.23x cropped region of the sensor — 6,240 pixels of 7,728 — not the full frame. 4K sub-sampled is full-width to 30p; 4K/60p sub-sampled costs a 1.14x crop. 6.2K and HQ top out at 30p — 60p only exists in the sub-sampled 4K modes. If the wide end of your lens matters, the sub-sampled 4K modes keep it.

STEP 2

The codec ladder

MENU→MOVIE SETTING→MEDIA RECORD SETTING

H.265 records 10-bit 4:2:2, H.264 is 8-bit; bit rates run 360/200/100/50 Mbps, and 360 needs a V60 card or better. There is no ProRes recording in this body — the ceiling is H.265 10-bit. For most work, H.265 at 200 Mbps is the sweet spot: full 10-bit color at a file size a single card can hold.

STEP 3

Log, graded right

MENU→MOVIE SETTING→F-Log/HLG RECORDING

F-Log starts at ISO 500, F-Log2 at ISO 1000, HLG at ISO 800 — all cap at 12800. F-Log2 holds more highlight range; HLG is built for HDR display. Turn on F-Log VIEW ASSIST or the monitor shows the flat log image. The micro-HDMI port outputs 12-bit RAW to Atomos and Blackmagic recorders — the frame size auto-changes to 6.2K and ISO is restricted to 800–12800.

STEP 4

Heat and silence

MENU→SET UP→POWER MANAGEMENT→AUTO POWER OFF TEMP.

High-speed recording runs Full HD at 100/120/200/240 fps — and high-speed movies are recorded with no sound. Heat is the real recording limit: about an hour of 4K30 or half an hour of 4K60 at room temperature before the camera stops. AUTO POWER OFF TEMP. on HIGH extends takes for tripod work; the optional FAN-001 fan buys more. There is no headphone jack — monitor with the supplied USB adapter.

TRY IT NOW

Set MOVIE MODE to 4K sub-sampled, H.265 10-bit at 200 Mbps, F-Log2, and record a 30-second test — then check the crop against 6.2K.
