Autofocus

What are the right autofocus settings on the Fujifilm X-T50 — tracking, subject detection, eye AF?

https://camerasdecoded.com/camera-select/fujifilm/x-t50/autofocus.html
THE ANSWER
Flick the focus mode selector on the front — S for still subjects, C for motion — pick your area with AF MODE, name the subject in SUBJECT DETECTION SETTING, and know the trade: turning subject detection on switches face/eye detection off, and turning face detection on switches subject detection off.
Focus mode: the S/C/M selector on the front · Area: AF/MF SETTING → AF MODE · Subject: AF/MF SETTING → SUBJECT DETECTION SETTING · Eye: AF/MF SETTING → FACE/EYE DETECTION SETTING
Free lesson — the guided run walks every step.
STEP 1

The lever, not a menu

MENU→AF/MF SETTING→AF MODE

The focus mode selector sits on the front of the body: S locks once for still subjects, C tracks motion, M hands focus to the ring. Then AF MODE picks the area (SINGLE POINT, ZONE, WIDE/TRACKING, ALL) and FOCUS AREA sets its position and size. The sensor offers 425 points (17×25) or 117 (9×13) — the choice applies in manual focus or with SINGLE POINT.

STEP 2

Name the subject — exactly

MENU→AF/MF SETTING→SUBJECT DETECTION SETTING

Subject detection hunts only what you name: ANIMAL, BIRD, AUTOMOBILE, MOTORCYCLE&BIKE, AIRPLANE, TRAIN. ANIMAL means dogs and cats only — birds have their own type, and other animals are not covered. Pick the type that matches, or the camera hunts the wrong thing.

STEP 3

Catch the moment before it happens

MENU→SHOOTING SETTING→PRE-SHOT ES

PRE-SHOT ES starts shooting with the electronic shutter at a half-press and saves shots starting just before the full press — it needs electronic shutter in CH (high-speed burst) drive mode, and flash is disabled. SPORTS FINDER MODE crops to 1.29x and shows the crop as a frame in the display, so you can see a subject entering the shot. AF-C CUSTOM SETTINGS tune how tracking behaves: SET 2 ignores obstacles, SET 3 follows accelerating and decelerating subjects, SET 6 is yours to build.

STEP 4

Eye AF — and the trade, both ways

MENU→AF/MF SETTING→FACE/EYE DETECTION SETTING

Face/eye detection finds faces fast, with EYE AUTO or a right- or left-eye priority — and Fn1 on the front ships as the FACE DETECTION ON/OFF toggle. But the two systems switch each other off in both directions: enabling subject detection turns face/eye detection OFF, and enabling face detection turns subject detection OFF. If eye AF stops working, check subject detection first.

TRY IT NOW

Flick the selector to C, set AF MODE to ZONE, turn on SUBJECT DETECTION for the subject you shoot most, and check what happened to face/eye detection.
