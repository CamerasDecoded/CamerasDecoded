Common Fixes

What fixes the Fujifilm X-S20's known problems — detection that switches off, recording that stops, flash that won't fire?

https://camerasdecoded.com/camera-select/fujifilm/x-s20/common-fixes.html
THE ANSWER
Eye AF that vanished means subject detection is on — the two systems switch each other off. A take that stops is heat or a slow card. A flash that won’t fire is the electronic shutter. And if the phone app won’t connect after firmware 3.30, the old Camera Remote app is done — XApp is the only way in now.
Eye AF: AF/MF SETTING → SUBJECT DETECTION SETTING → OFF · Heat: SET UP → POWER MANAGEMENT → AUTO POWER OFF TEMP. → HIGH · Flash: SHOOTING SETTING → SHUTTER TYPE → MECHANICAL · App: install FUJIFILM XApp
Free lesson — the guided run walks every step.
STEP 1

One system turns the other off

MENU→AF/MF SETTING→SUBJECT DETECTION SETTING

Eye AF finds no faces because subject detection is on — enabling it automatically switches FACE/EYE DETECTION to OFF. Turn subject detection OFF and eye AF comes back immediately. If you shoot people and animals in the same session, put both toggles on buttons.

STEP 2

Card first, then heat

MENU→SET UP→POWER MANAGEMENT→AUTO POWER OFF TEMP.

A take that stops is the card first: 360 Mbps needs V60 or better, and a slower card stops the recording without warning. Then heat: the camera warns, then ends shooting and powers down. AUTO POWER OFF TEMP. on HIGH buys time, the FAN-001 buys more, and the 1080/60P LP mode runs coolest.

STEP 3

Blame the electronic shutter

MENU→SHOOTING SETTING→SHUTTER TYPE

The pop-up flash will not fire when the electronic shutter is used — set SHUTTER TYPE to MECHANICAL SHUTTER (MECHANICAL + ELECTRONIC can still choose the electronic shutter). It also will not fire in panorama mode, or with SOUND & FLASH off. In S or M mode, keep the shutter speed slower than 1/180 s.

STEP 4

The app that stopped connecting

MENU→SET UP→USER SETTING→FIRMWARE UPDATE

Firmware 3.30 changed the wireless system for security: the old Camera Remote app no longer connects, the WIRELESS COMMUNICATION menu item is gone, and there is no rollback. Install FUJIFILM XApp and pair again. And if the frame number reaches 999-9999, the shutter is disabled — power off and insert a formatted card.

TRY IT NOW

Turn subject detection off and watch eye AF return; set AUTO POWER OFF TEMP. to HIGH; switch SHUTTER TYPE to MECHANICAL and test the pop-up flash.
