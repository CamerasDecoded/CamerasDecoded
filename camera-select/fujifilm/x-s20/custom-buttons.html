Custom Buttons

Which buttons should you customize on the Fujifilm X-S20, and what belongs on each one?

https://camerasdecoded.com/camera-select/fujifilm/x-s20/custom-buttons.html
THE ANSWER
This body has one Fn button and one Fn dial — so every assignment counts. Put SUBJECT DETECTION ON/OFF and FACE DETECTION ON/OFF on buttons, because the menu buries the swap you make most; leave the Fn dial on film simulation until you know what you want instead.
Assign: hold DISP/BACK, or BUTTON/DIAL SETTING → FUNCTION (Fn) SETTING · Fn dial: BUTTON/DIAL SETTING → FUNCTION (Fn) SETTING · Save: IMAGE QUALITY SETTING → EDIT/SAVE CUSTOM SETTING → CUSTOM 1
Pro lesson — the deep steps are in the guided run.
STEP 1

Know the factory map first

MENU→BUTTON/DIAL SETTING→FUNCTION (Fn) SETTING

Six controls, one Fn button: the movie button records, ISO sets sensitivity, Q opens the quick menu, Fn ships as WHITE BALANCE, AEL locks exposure only, and AFON is autofocus. There is no D-pad — the focus stick moves the focus point. Press and hold DISP/BACK to reassign any of them.

STEP 2

Build the detection swap

MENU→BUTTON/DIAL SETTING→FUNCTION (Fn) SETTING

Subject detection and face/eye detection switch each other off, so the toggle between them is the one you reach for mid-shoot. Put SUBJECT DETECTION ON/OFF on the Fn button and FACE DETECTION ON/OFF on a second button — one press beats three menus when the subject changes.

STEP 3

Two more that earn their place

MENU→BUTTON/DIAL SETTING→FUNCTION (Fn) SETTING

Video shooters: IS MODE BOOST, HIGH SPEED REC, F-Log VIEW ASSIST, COOLING FAN SETTING. Vloggers: PRODUCT PRIORITY MODE and BACKGROUND DEFOCUS MODE. Action shooters: PRE-SHOT ES and SPORTS FINDER MODE. RECALL CUSTOM 1 SETTING temporarily recalls the C1 bank while held.

STEP 4

What the map survives

MENU→IMAGE QUALITY SETTING→EDIT/SAVE CUSTOM SETTING

The Fn dial ships as a film-sim dial (filter selection in FILTER mode) — its role is changeable in FUNCTION (Fn) SETTING. Save the whole map with EDIT/SAVE CUSTOM SETTING to CUSTOM 1–4, recalled from the mode dial. Leave AUTO UPDATE CUSTOM SETTING DISABLED, or casual changes rewrite the bank you are standing in.

TRY IT NOW

Hold DISP/BACK, put SUBJECT DETECTION ON/OFF on the Fn button, and save the map to CUSTOM 1.
