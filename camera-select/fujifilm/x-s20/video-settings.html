Video Settings

What are the right video settings on the Fujifilm X-S20 — 6.2K open gate, 4K60, F-Log2 and slow motion?

https://camerasdecoded.com/camera-select/fujifilm/x-s20/video-settings.html
THE ANSWER
Shoot 6.2K open gate for the best file — full sensor, uncropped, 10-bit 4:2:2 — and know that 4K/60p costs a 1.18x crop. Grade with F-Log2 at ISO 1250 or higher, use Vlog mode for talking-head work, and feed it a V60 card: 360 Mbps is the ceiling and the card has to keep up.
Mode: MOVIE SETTING → MOVIE MODE · Log: MOVIE SETTING → F-Log/HLG RECORDING · Vlog: mode dial → Vlog · Heat: SET UP → POWER MANAGEMENT → AUTO POWER OFF TEMP.
Pro lesson — the deep steps are in the guided run.
STEP 1

6.2K, or 4K done properly

MENU→MOVIE SETTING→MOVIE MODE

The 3:2 mode records 6.2K open gate — the full sensor, uncropped, 10-bit 4:2:2 at up to 30p. 4K/60p takes a 1.18x crop, and the other 4K modes take the same crop in 10-bit. Codecs are H.265 10-bit or H.264 8-bit at 360/200/100/50 Mbps, and 360 Mbps needs a V60 card or better.

STEP 2

Two logs, two floors

MENU→MOVIE SETTING→F-Log/HLG RECORDING

F-Log starts at ISO 640; F-Log2 starts at ISO 1250 and holds more highlight range; HLG starts at ISO 1000 and is built for HDR display. All three cap at ISO 12800. Turn on F-Log VIEW ASSIST or the monitor shows the flat log image. The micro-HDMI port outputs RAW to Atomos and Blackmagic recorders at 6.2K/30p.

STEP 3

240 frames, and silent

MENU→MOVIE SETTING→HIGH SPEED REC

High-speed recording runs at 240, 200, 120 or 100 frames a second, with separate record and playback rates and an HDMI ONLY option. High-speed movies are recorded with no sound — plan audio separately.

STEP 4

Plan heat, not minutes

MENU→SET UP→POWER MANAGEMENT→AUTO POWER OFF TEMP.

AUTO POWER OFF TEMP. on HIGH lets shooting continue at higher temperatures — tripod work, since the body gets hot to hold. The optional FAN-001 fan buys more time, and the 1080/60P LP mode trades a 1.29x crop for a much cooler sensor: 32 minutes at 40 °C, 78 minutes with the fan. The battery is the other clock — about 85 minutes of actual recording at 6.2K.

TRY IT NOW

Set MOVIE MODE to 6.2K, F-Log2 on, AUTO POWER OFF TEMP. to HIGH, and record a test clip — then check the card kept up.
