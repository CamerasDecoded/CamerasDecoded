Autofocus

What are the right autofocus settings on the Fujifilm X-S20 — tracking, subject detection, eye AF?

https://camerasdecoded.com/camera-select/fujifilm/x-s20/autofocus.html
THE ANSWER
Set FOCUS MODE in the menu — there is no lever on this body — AF-S with SINGLE POINT for still subjects, AF-C with ZONE or WIDE/TRACKING for motion, subject detection for animals, birds and vehicles, and know the trade: turning subject detection on switches face/eye detection off.
Focus mode: AF/MF SETTING → FOCUS MODE · Area: AF/MF SETTING → AF MODE · Subject: AF/MF SETTING → SUBJECT DETECTION SETTING · Eye: AF/MF SETTING → FACE/EYE DETECTION SETTING
Free lesson — the guided run walks every step.
STEP 1

The menu, not a lever

MENU→AF/MF SETTING→FOCUS MODE

There is no S/C/M lever on this body — FOCUS MODE lives in the menu: SINGLE AF locks once, CONTINUOUS AF tracks motion, MANUAL FOCUS hands it to the ring. Then AF MODE picks the area (SINGLE POINT, ZONE, WIDE/TRACKING, ALL) and FOCUS AREA sets its position and size. The sensor offers 425 points (17×25) or 117 (9×13).

STEP 2

Name the subject — exactly

MENU→AF/MF SETTING→SUBJECT DETECTION SETTING

Subject detection hunts only what you name: ANIMAL, BIRD, AUTOMOBILE, MOTORCYCLE&BIKE, AIRPLANE, TRAIN. ANIMAL means dogs and cats only — birds have their own type, and other animals are not covered. Pick the type that matches, or the camera hunts the wrong thing.

STEP 3

Catch the moment before it happens

MENU→SHOOTING SETTING→PRE-SHOT ES

PRE-SHOT ES starts shooting with the electronic shutter at a half-press and saves shots starting just before the full press — it needs electronic shutter in CH (high-speed burst) drive mode. SPORTS FINDER MODE crops to 1.29x and shows the crop as a frame in the display, so you can see a subject entering the shot. For video, AF-C CUSTOM SETTINGS tune the movie autofocus: TRACKING SENSITIVITY 0–4 and AF SPEED −5 to +5.

STEP 4

Eye AF — and the trade

MENU→AF/MF SETTING→FACE/EYE DETECTION SETTING

Face/eye detection finds faces fast, and a function button can switch between the right and left eye. But enabling subject detection automatically switches FACE/EYE DETECTION to OFF — one system at a time, and the camera makes the swap silently. If eye AF stops working, check subject detection first.

TRY IT NOW

Set FOCUS MODE to CONTINUOUS AF, AF MODE to ZONE, turn on SUBJECT DETECTION for the subject you shoot most, and check what happened to face/eye detection.
