Custom button map

How do you assign the custom controls on a Nikon Z6 III — and which functions can each button actually hold?

camerasdecoded.com/camera-select/nikon/z6-iii/custom-buttons
The answer — share this
Put AF-area mode + AF-ON on Fn1 so a held button grabs 3D-tracking, Switch eyes on Fn2, and Choose image area on the video record button for instant DX reach — all assigned in f2.
Custom Settings Menu → f2 Custom controls (shooting)  ·  Fn1: AF-area mode + AF-ON (3D-tracking)  ·  Fn2: Switch eyes  ·  Video record button: Choose image area  ·  Save the setup to U1
Cameras Decoded — Camera Select
Step 1

Work at the f2 desk

MENU→Custom Settings Menu→f2 Custom controls (shooting)

All stills assignments are made at Custom Setting f2: highlight a control, press the center button, choose a role. The bench includes Fn1, Fn2, AF-ON, the sub-selector center, the video record button, the command dials, and controls on the lens. Video assignments live separately at g2 Custom controls — a map built for stills does not follow you into video mode.

Step 2

Fn1 — the tracking grab

MENU→Custom Settings Menu→f2 Custom controls (shooting)

Assign Fn1 the role AF-area mode + AF-ON and preset its area to 3D-tracking. Holding Fn1 switches the camera to that area and starts autofocus; releasing it restores the AF-area mode you were using. It is a borrow, not a switch — built for the seconds when your everyday area loses the subject.

Step 3

Fn2 — switch eyes; video button — DX reach

MENU→Custom Settings Menu→f2 Custom controls (shooting)

Fn2 takes Switch eyes: with a human or animal subject detected, a press moves focus between the subject's eyes. The video record button — idle in photo mode — takes Choose image area: press and turn a command dial to move between FX and the DX crop for instant reach. Both roles come straight from Nikon's f2 table.

Know this: there is no Subject detection role in f2 on this body. Detection categories change in the Photo/Video Recording Menus or through the i menu item AF-area mode/subj. detection — any button map that promises otherwise was not built from Nikon's table.
Step 4

Save the map to U1

MENU→Setup Menu→Save user settings

Register the finished map to U1 so a reset or a firmware day cannot take it. If your video work matters, spend two minutes at g2 Custom controls mirroring the roles you rely on — the maps are independent.

Try it now

Assign the three roles, then hold Fn1 on a walking subject and release it. The override you feel is the map working.
