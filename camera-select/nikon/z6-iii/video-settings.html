Video settings, in depth

What are the right video settings on a Nikon Z6 III — file types, 6K and 4K frame rates, and N-Log?

camerasdecoded.com/camera-select/nikon/z6-iii/video-settings
The answer — share this
Shoot H.265 10-bit at 4K 60p for everyday work, N-Log at ISO 800 when you will grade, and know the ladder above it: 5.4K, 4K 120p, and 6K N-RAW.
Video file type: H.265 10-bit (MOV)  ·  Frame size/frame rate: 3840×2160 60p  ·  Tone mode: N-Log when grading  ·  View assist: Custom Settings g13 → ON  ·  Destination: CFexpress/XQD slot
Cameras Decoded — Camera Select
Step 1

Choose the codec before the size

MENU→Video Recording Menu→Video file type

Video file type decides which frame sizes exist, so it is chosen first. H.265 10-bit (MOV) is the working default. ProRes 422 HQ 10-bit serves edit-heavy workflows. N-RAW 12-bit (NEV) and ProRes RAW HQ 12-bit (MOV) record true RAW — at the cost of Electronic VR, in-camera playback/editing conveniences, and HDMI output above 1080p while selected. N-RAW also has its own quality switch: Video quality (N-RAW), High quality or Normal.

Step 2

Climb the frame-size ladder deliberately

MENU→Video Recording Menu→Frame size/frame rate

3840×2160 (4K UHD) at 60p is the everyday rung and exists in every file type; 4K reaches 120p in ProRes 422 HQ and H.265, and Full HD reaches 240p in H.265 for deep slow motion. 5376×3024 (5.4K) records at 30p in H.265 and only reaches 60p in ProRes 422 HQ. 6048×3402 (6K) belongs to the RAW types — 60p and 50p in N-RAW only — and the RAW table adds a DX-format 3984×2240 option that reaches 120p in N-RAW.

Step 3

N-Log is a tone mode

MENU→Video Recording Menu→Tone mode

Select N-Log as the Tone mode with a 10-bit or RAW file type active — it cannot be chosen with 8-bit types. Nikon states 13+ stops (1300%) of dynamic range for H.265 10-bit with ISO sensitivity at its lowest, ISO 800. While N-Log is on, Set Picture Control, Active D-Lighting, High ISO NR, Skin softening and Portrait impression balance are unavailable, and Hi ISO settings close. It is footage meant to be graded; Nikon's N-Log LUTs are free from the Nikon Download Center. Custom Setting g13, View assist, restores contrast to the preview only.

Know this: a flat, grey preview is N-Log working as designed. Judge exposure by the tools, not by how the monitor looks — and leave View assist on so the preview stays readable.
Step 4

Destination and heat discipline

MENU→Video Recording Menu→Destination

Videos record to the slot chosen at Destination when two cards are inserted — stills slot settings do not govern them. Send RAW and high-rate work to the CFexpress/XQD slot on a fast card (Nikon recommends at least 250 MB/s for N-RAW and ProRes RAW). Each shot may run up to 125 minutes, and a 660 GB card holds about 198 minutes of H.265 8-bit at 5.4K 60p or 4K 120p — but the manual warns recording may end sooner if the battery fades or the camera's temperature rises. When the high-temperature warning appears, stop; the body, battery and cards may be hot, and cards come out only after the warning clears.

Try it now

Build the chain end to end — H.265 10-bit, 4K 60p, N-Log, View assist, CFexpress destination — and shoot the test clip. Flat is correct.
