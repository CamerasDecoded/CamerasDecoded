Autofocus, set up right

How do you set up autofocus on a Nikon Z30 — 209 focus points, AF-C for motion, eye detection for people and animals (dogs and cats only — no birds, no vehicles), subject tracking on the OK button, and no AF-assist lamp when it gets dark?

camerasdecoded.com/camera-select/nikon/z30/autofocus
The answer — share this
Set AF-C for anything that moves and AF-S for what holds still, pick the area mode to match the shot, let eye detection find people and pets — and in the dark, turn on low-light AF (a10), because this body has no AF-assist lamp at all.
Focus mode: AF-S still / AF-C moving / AF-A auto-switch (photo) / AF-F video  ·  Points: 209  ·  Eye detection: people + dogs/cats only — no birds, no vehicles  ·  Tracking: OK button in auto-area modes  ·  Low light: Custom Setting a10 Low-light AF (−4.5 EV)  ·  No AF-assist lamp on this body  ·  Defaults: hold Fn2 + main dial = focus mode, hold Fn2 + sub dial = area mode
Cameras Decoded — Camera Select
Step 1

Match the mode to the subject

MENU→i menu→Focus mode

AF-S (single-servo) focuses once and locks — for what holds still, and the shutter will not fire until the camera has focus. AF-C (continuous-servo) keeps focusing while you hold the shutter halfway, with predictive tracking — for anything that moves, and the shutter fires whether or not the subject is in focus. AF-A (auto-servo, photos only) starts in AF-S and switches to AF-C if the subject moves. AF-F (full-time-servo) is video only. Change them the fast way: hold Fn2 and rotate the main command dial.

Step 2

Pick the area to match the shot

MENU→i menu→AF-area mode

Pinpoint AF (photos only, AF-S only) is the smallest area — macro, product work, precise placement. Single-point AF is the deliberate choice: one point, where you put it. Dynamic-area AF (photos only, AF-A/AF-C) is a point with helpers. Wide-area AF (S) and (L) are zones for motion; a zone with subjects at different distances prioritizes the closest. Auto-area AF finds the subject itself. Change it the fast way: hold Fn2 and rotate the sub-command dial. Custom Setting a7 (Limit AF-area mode selection) hides the area modes you never use.

Step 3

Eyes for people and pets only

MENU→Photo Shooting Menu→AF-area mode

Wide-area AF (L-people) or Auto-area AF (people) finds human faces and eyes; the (animals) versions find dogs and cats. A yellow border appears around a detected face, a yellow point over the eye. In AF-C the point stays yellow; in AF-S it turns green when focus locks. With more than one face or eye, the 4 and 2 buttons move between them. Eye detection works in stills and video. It can miss when a face is very large or small in the frame, badly lit, obscured by hair or fur, or moving fast — and there is no bird detection and no vehicle detection on this body.

Step 4

Track with OK

Subject tracking lives in the auto-area modes: press OK for the targeting reticle, position it over the subject, press OK again to start tracking; press OK again to end and return to the center point, or press the W (Q) button to exit tracking mode. In photo mode with AF-C, tracking lasts only while the shutter is held halfway. Touch AF tracks the subject you tap in auto-area modes. Custom Setting a4 (Focus points used) set to Alternating points uses only a quarter of the points for faster point selection.

Step 5

The dark has no lamp

MENU→Custom Settings Menu→a10 Low-light AF

This body has no AF-assist lamp — nothing lights the subject for the camera. What helps is Custom Setting a10 (Low-light AF), which takes the detection floor from -3 EV to -4.5 EV in AF-S (photo mode only; Low-light appears in the display and focusing may take longer). It is fixed On in AUTO mode and disabled during interval-timer photography and time-lapse video. If it still will not lock, manual focus with peaking is the honest answer.

Try it now

Hold Fn2 and roll the dials through the focus modes and area modes. Set Auto-area AF (people) and watch the yellow point find an eye. Press OK and track someone across the room. Turn on Low-light AF (a10) and leave it on.
