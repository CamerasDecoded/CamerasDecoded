Common fixes

What do you do when a Nikon Z30 freezes on power-on, stops a 4K take early, will not focus in the dark, or locks every button with the monitor flipped forward?

camerasdecoded.com/camera-select/nikon/z30/common-fixes
The answer — share this
A Z30 that freezes on power-on needs firmware C 1.20; a 4K take that stops early is heat, and the REC lamp flashes four times fast to say so; autofocus that gives up in the dark needs low-light AF (a10), because there is no assist lamp; and buttons that go dead with the monitor flipped forward are self-portrait mode doing its job.
Freeze on power-on: update to C 1.20  ·  4K stops early: heat — about 35 min; REC lamp 4× fast = hot  ·  Dark focus: a10 Low-light AF (−4.5 EV); no AF-assist lamp  ·  Wrong face: 4/2 buttons move between faces  ·  Buttons dead: self-portrait mode — Setup Menu → Self-portrait mode → Disable  ·  Burst slows: buffer full, r00 — 14-bit 36 / 12-bit 44 / JPEG fine 82  ·  Banding: silent photography + flickering light — turn silent off
Cameras Decoded — Camera Select
1Stops responding on power-on. You open the monitor from its stored position, turn the camera on, and it freezes — only when Bluetooth pairing or the Wi-Fi connection is on. That is a firmware bug, and C 1.20 (May 2025) fixed it. Check Setup Menu → Firmware version and update via the Nikon Download Center, SnapBridge, or Nikon Imaging Cloud. If it freezes before you can update, remove the battery for ten seconds and update with the monitor already open.
2A 4K take stops early. Heat. The 125-minute figure is Full HD — at 4K, a take runs about 35 minutes before the camera stops it to protect itself. The REC lamp tells you first: flashing 4 times quickly (repeating) means the temperature inside the camera is rising. Let it cool, shoot shorter takes, keep it out of direct sun. Slow flashing means the card is nearly full; twice quickly (repeating) means the battery is low.
3Autofocus gives up in the dark. This body has no AF-assist lamp — there is nothing to turn on. Turn on Custom Setting a10 (Low-light AF), which takes the detection floor from -3 EV to -4.5 EV in AF-S; it costs nothing in daylight, so leave it on. If it still will not lock, find an edge with contrast, open the aperture, or switch to manual focus with peaking (Custom Setting d8). AF-C is worse in the dark, not better.
4Eye detection picks the wrong face. Press the 4 and 2 buttons to move between detected faces and eyes. If it will not find your dog, check the area mode — a people mode will not look for a dog; switch to Wide-area AF (L-animals) or Auto-area AF (animals). Animal detection can miss in poor light, when fur hides the eyes, or when the animal moves fast. This body has no bird or vehicle detection — for those, single-point AF and your own aim are the tools.
5Every button goes dead with the monitor forward. That is self-portrait mode: only the power switch, shutter-release button, video-record button, photo/video selector and mode dial stay live. Touch controls still work for the self-timer and exposure compensation. If you want the monitor forward without the lock, turn it off at Setup Menu → Self-portrait mode → Disable.
6A burst slows down mid-shoot. The buffer is full — the camera displays r00 and the frame advance rate drops. RAW at 14 bits gives 36 frames (12-bit gives 44, JPEG fine gives 82). Custom Setting d2 (Maximum shots per burst) caps at 100, and there is no limit at 1 s or slower in S/M or during silent photography. Shoot 12-bit or JPEG when the burst matters more than the last bit of color.
7Banding in silent photography. The electronic shutter met flickering light — fluorescent, mercury vapor, or sodium lamps. Turn silent photography off, or switch the shutter type (d4) back to mechanical. Silent photography also disables the flash, long exposure noise reduction, flicker reduction and Hi 1/Hi 2, and it does not completely silence the camera — AF and aperture sounds remain, notably below f/5.6.
8The battery dies too fast. CIPA ratings are about 360 shots with the EN-EL25a and about 330 with the EN-EL25 (video: about 85 vs 75 minutes) — and the EN-EL25a needs firmware C 1.10 or newer. The monitor is the finder, so it is always on: lower the monitor brightness, shorten the standby timer (Custom Setting c3), turn off Wi-Fi and Bluetooth when you are not transferring, and carry a spare. USB-C charging works with the EH-7P/EH-8P adapters and with power banks.
Try it now

Check the firmware reads C 1.20. Turn on Low-light AF (a10) and leave it on. Flip the monitor forward, feel the buttons lock, then disable self-portrait mode and feel them come back. Shoot a burst until r00 appears — so the first time you see it is not the time it matters.
