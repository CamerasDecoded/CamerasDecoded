Common fixes

What do you do when your Z fc will not cooperate — dials that fight you, autofocus that gives up in the dark, eye detection on the wrong face, or a monitor that stays blank?

camerasdecoded.com/camera-select/nikon/zfc/common-fixes
The answer — share this
A shutter speed that will not change is the dial, not the camera — turn it to 1/3STEP. Autofocus that gives up in the dark needs low-light AF. Eye detection on the wrong face is one press of 4 or 2 away. A monitor that stays blank with Prioritize viewfinder is firmware 1.81.
Shutter speed stuck: dial → 1/3STEP (lock release for X/T)  ·  Dark focus: a10 Low-light AF, −4.5 EV  ·  Wrong face: 4/2 buttons move between faces  ·  Blank monitor: update to C 1.81  ·  Burst slows: buffer full — RAW 14-bit is 36 frames  ·  Banding: silent photography + flickering light

The shutter speed will not changeThe dial has the last word. The shutter-speed dial is set to a fixed speed, and a fixed speed is a fixed speed: rolling the command dial does nothing. Turn the shutter-speed dial to 1/3STEP and the main command dial takes over in 1/3-stop steps, down to 30 seconds. If the dial itself will not move, press the lock release: it guards X and T, and the way out of 1/3STEP, X, T and B. Two more dial traps: in AUTO mode the ISO dial is fixed at ISO-A — the dial does nothing until you leave AUTO — and in video mode the ISO dial only works in mode M with Auto ISO control (mode M) off, with dial values above 25600 clamping to 25600.
Autofocus gives up in the darkTurn on low-light AF (a10). Custom Setting a10 Low-light AF takes the detection floor from -3 EV down to -4.5 EV — the difference between focusing in a dim room and hunting forever. Turn it on and leave it on. If it still hunts, check the AF-assist illuminator: it reaches only about 1 to 3 meters, and a lens hood can block it — take the hood off in the dark, or get closer. If the subject is an animal, the AF-assist light can bother its eyes — Custom Setting a11 turns the illuminator off.
Eye detection on the wrong facePress 4 or 2 to switch. With more than one face or eye in the frame, the 4 and 2 buttons move between them. If it will not find your dog at all, check the area mode — a people mode will not look for a dog, and an animals mode will not prioritize a person. Switch to Wide-area AF (L-animals) or Auto-area AF (animals). If animal detection still misses, the manual names the causes: the face is very large or very small in the frame, the light is poor, fur hides the eyes, the eyes are close in color to the face, or the animal is moving fast. And if you are pointing at a bird or a car — this body has no bird or vehicle detection.
The monitor stays blankUpdate to firmware C 1.81. A monitor that stays blank with Prioritize viewfinder selected for monitor mode is firmware 1.81 — the June 2026 update fixed exactly that issue. Check Setup Menu → Firmware version and update if you are behind; updates come via computer from the Nikon Download Center, via the SnapBridge app, or via Nikon Imaging Cloud.
The burst slows downThe buffer is finite — r00. RAW at 14 bits gives you 36 frames before the camera displays r00 and the frame advance rate drops (12-bit gives 44, JPEG fine gives 83). A UHS-I card can only clear it so fast. The fixes: shoot 12-bit or JPEG for long bursts, or raise Custom Setting d2 Maximum shots per burst — and know it caps at 100.
Banding in silent photographyThe electronic shutter met flickering light. Stripes across the frame in silent photography mean the electronic shutter met flickering light — fluorescent, mercury or sodium lamps. Silent photography also disables the flash, long exposure noise reduction, flicker reduction and Hi 1/Hi 2, and it does not completely silence the camera — AF and aperture sounds remain, notably at apertures smaller than f/5.6. Turn silent photography off, or switch the shutter type (d4) back to mechanical.
The battery dies fastAbout 360 shots — carry a spare. CIPA rates the EN-EL25a at about 360 shots (viewfinder) or 400 (monitor); the older EN-EL25 manages about 310/360. Video runs about 90 minutes on the EN-EL25a. The EN-EL25a needs firmware C 1.50 or newer. Charge over USB-C with an EH-7P or EH-8P adapter, or a portable charger — and shorten the standby timer (c3 Power off delay) if the camera sits idle between shots.
Try it now

Check the firmware — current is C 1.81 — turn on a10 Low-light AF, and try the 4 and 2 buttons on two faces. Then shoot a burst at Continuous H (extended), 11 fps, and watch the buffer count down to r00.
