Video settings

How do you set up video on a Nikon Z fc — 4K UHD at 30p with no crop at 144 megabits, slow motion at 120 frames, Electronic VR that crops, and no log profile at all?

The answer — share this
4K UHD at 30p, full pixel readout with no crop, 144 megabits at High quality — Electronic VR only when you need it, because it crops the angle of view — and no log profile on this body: the Flat Picture Control is the flat option.
Frame size and rate: 3840×2160; 30p  ·  Quality: 144 Mbps, High (fixed)  ·  Electronic VR: On only when needed — crops the frame  ·  Mic: Manual 1–20 + Attenuator  ·  Slow motion: 1080 30p ×4, no sound  ·  Log: none — Flat Picture Control instead  ·  REC frame: g7 Red REC frame indicator

Step 1

4K at 30p, no crop, 144 megabits

MENU→Movie Shooting Menu→Frame size and rate→Video quality

Choose the frame size for the job. 4K UHD (3840×2160) at 30p, 25p or 24p is full pixel readout with no cropping — the whole DX width, not a window in the middle of the sensor. The bitrate is 144 Mbps and the quality is fixed at High: there is no Normal quality to fall back to at 4K, and no way to shrink the file except by dropping to 1080p. At 1080p you get 120p and 100p (quality fixed at High), 60p and 50p at 56 Mbps High / 28 Normal, and 30p/25p/24p at 28 High / 14 Normal. Every take stops at 29 minutes 59 seconds. And one card fact: cards over 32 GB record to the maximum length in a single file; cards of 32 GB or less may split a long take into up to 8 files of up to 4 GB each.

Step 2

Electronic VR crops — use it on purpose

MENU→Movie Shooting Menu→Electronic VR

Two stabilizers in video, and they behave differently. Lens VR (Vibration reduction in the Movie Shooting Menu) steadies the optics, as it does for stills. Electronic VR is digital stabilization — and it reduces the angle of view, slightly increasing the apparent focal length. Use it when you are handholding and need the steadiness; turn it off when the framing matters more, or when the camera is on a tripod. And know its limits: Electronic VR is disabled at 1080 120p/100p and in slow-motion. The autofocus is full-time: AF-F keeps focusing while you record, and the people and animals area modes bring eye detection to video — the same people-and-pets coverage as stills, no birds, no vehicles. g3 AF speed and g4 AF tracking sensitivity tune how fast and how stubbornly video AF follows.

Step 3

True slow motion, no sound

MENU→Movie Shooting Menu→Frame size and rate→1080p; 30p ×4 (slow-motion)

Slow motion is real slow motion, not a trick. 1080 30p ×4 reads at 120p and plays at 30p — 10 seconds recorded becomes about 40 seconds of playback. 25p ×4 reads at 100p; 24p ×5 reads at 120p. The bitrate is 36 Mbps and a slow-motion take stops at 3 minutes. Three limits to know: sound is not recorded in slow motion; face and eye detection switch off in the people and animals AF-area modes; and flicker reduction, Electronic VR, HDMI output while recording and timecode recording are all disabled. The screen may flicker under fluorescent light while recording slow motion. And one more honesty: there is no log profile on this body — no N-Log, no HLG. If you want flat footage to grade, the Flat Picture Control is the flat option; the 20 Creative Picture Controls are the in-camera looks.

Step 4

Set the mic — you cannot hear it live

MENU→Movie Shooting Menu→Microphone sensitivity
MENU→Custom Settings Menu→g7→Red REC frame indicator

Microphone sensitivity offers Auto, Manual 1–20, or Microphone off — and a red sound level means too high. The Attenuator reduces gain in loud environments; Wind noise reduction tames outdoor hiss. The built-in stereo mic works; the 3.5 mm stereo mini-pin jack takes an external mic with plug-in power. And the honest gap: there is no headphone jack on this body, so you cannot monitor audio while you record. Set the level, watch the meter, record a test clip and play it back. Two finishing touches: g7 Red REC frame indicator (firmware 1.60) puts a red frame around the display while recording, so you never wonder whether you are rolling — and video ISO runs 100–25600, with the ISO dial working only in mode M with Auto ISO control (mode M) off, and dial values above 25600 clamping to 25600.

Try it now

Record one 4K 30p clip and one slow-motion clip — then play them back and check the file sizes on the card. Turn on the Red REC frame (g7), flip the screen forward to try self-portrait mode, and record a test clip with the mic set manually — then play it back and listen, because there is no headphone jack to check with live.
