Autofocus, set up right

How do you set up autofocus and subject detection on a Nikon Zf — including detection that works with manual-focus lenses?

camerasdecoded.com/camera-select/nikon/zf/autofocus
The answer — share this
Run AF-C with Wide-area AF (C1) sized to your subject and Subject detection set to People — or Auto when the subject keeps changing — and turn on MF subject detection so even manual-focus lenses get a detection box.
Focus mode: AF-C  ·  AF-area mode: Wide-area AF (C1) or Auto-area AF  ·  Subject detection: People or Auto  ·  MF glass: MF subject detection area → All  ·  Set at: Photo Shooting Menu → AF/MF subject detection options
Cameras Decoded — Camera Select
Step 1

AF-C for movers, AF-S for stills

MENU→Photo Shooting Menu→Focus mode

AF-C keeps adjusting focus while focus is held active — the mode for people, animals and anything alive. AF-S locks once and holds it: landscapes, still life, posed work. Video uses AF-F. For speed: continuous high runs about 7.8 fps, continuous high (extended) about 14 fps, and high-speed frame capture + records JPEG bursts at 30 fps (15 fps on current firmware), with Pre-Release Capture (Custom Setting d3) keeping frames from before the full press.

Step 2

Name the subject

MENU→Photo Shooting Menu→AF/MF subject detection options

The detection choices are Auto, People, Animal, Vehicle, Airplanes, and Subject detection off, chosen separately for photo and video. Animal means a dog, cat or bird. Vehicle is one bucket — cars, motorcycles, trains, airplanes and bicycles — while the separate Airplanes choice reads an aircraft's body, nose or cockpit. Firmware 2.00 added Birds as its own option; if your camera doesn't list it, update the firmware. People and Animal detection include eye detection.

Step 3

Wide-area AF (C1) — a zone you size

MENU→Photo Shooting Menu→AF-area mode

Subject detection runs in Wide-area AF (S), Wide-area AF (L), Wide-area AF (C1), Wide-area AF (C2), 3D-tracking and Auto-area AF — not in Single-point, Pinpoint or Dynamic-area AF. The C1/C2 zones are sized by the photographer: in FX for stills there are 77 sizes from 1×1 to 21×13 focus points, chosen with the arrow keys when the mode is selected. Size the zone to the subject — a head-and-shoulders box for portraits — and use 3D-tracking for subjects crossing the whole frame.

Step 4

Detection with manual focus

MENU→Photo Shooting Menu→MF subject detection area

With a manual-focus lens mounted, set MF subject detection area to All and the camera keeps detecting the chosen subject class across the whole frame, placing a focus point over it — over the eye for People — while you focus by hand. Wide (S) and Wide (L) limit detection to the current focus area. It is the feature that makes adapted vintage glass practical on this body, and no other body on this shelf offers it.

Try it now

Set AF-C, People detection and a head-and-shoulders C1 zone, and track a person across the room. Then fit a manual lens, set MF subject detection area to All, and focus by hand while the camera marks the eye.
