Custom button map

How do you assign the custom controls on a Nikon Z50 II — and which role does no button on this body get?

camerasdecoded.com/camera-select/nikon/z50-ii/custom-buttons
The answer — share this
Assign in f2: Fn1 takes AF-area mode + AF-ON — a held override that summons a preset area and starts focus — Fn2 takes Switch eyes, and the lens buttons pair Save focus position with Recall focus position. No button on this body can carry Subject detection: the fifth Nikon on this shelf without the role.
Custom Settings Menu → f2 Custom controls (shooting)  ·  Fn1 button: AF-area mode + AF-ON  ·  Fn2 button: Switch eyes  ·  Lens Fn2: Save focus position  ·  Lens Fn: Recall focus position  ·  Detection switches at: the shooting menus or the i menu
Cameras Decoded — Camera Select
Step 1

The f2 desk

MENU→Custom Settings Menu→f2 Custom controls (shooting)

All stills assignments happen at Custom Setting f2; there is no separate control-assignment menu on this body. The bench: Fn1 and Fn2 on the front (factory roles: Fn1 white balance, Fn2 Focus mode/AF-area mode, both adjusted by holding the button and turning the command dials), the DISP, AE-L/AF-L, Playback and OK buttons, the release-mode and video-record buttons, the ISO sensitivity and exposure compensation buttons, the dedicated Picture Control button, both command dials, and the lens controls — Lens Fn, Lens Fn2, the Lens Fn ring in both directions, the lens memory set button and the lens control ring. Video assignments live separately at g2 Custom controls, which can also assign the shutter-release button. Letter shift: [Reverse dial rotation] is f6 on this body.

Step 2

An override under your finger

MENU→Custom Settings Menu→f2 Custom controls (shooting)

Assign the Fn1 button AF-area mode + AF-ON with a preset of 3D-tracking: holding it swaps in the preset area and starts focus; releasing restores the area previously in effect. Assign Fn2 Switch eyes to choose the other eye when the camera detects a portrait subject. On lenses with the controls, pair Lens Fn2 → Save focus position (hold to store a focus distance) with Lens Fn → Recall focus position (one press returns focus to the stored mark).

Step 3

No detection button — five Nikons running

MENU→Custom Settings Menu→f2 Custom controls (shooting)

Subject detection has no assignable role in the f2 or g2 tables on this body — the fifth Nikon on this shelf without it. It is switched in [AF/MF subject detection options] in the photo and video shooting menus, or via the i menu mid-shoot. Set the day's default category in the menus and let the i menu carry the changes.

Try it now

Make the three assignments in f2, then shoot ten frames using the Fn1 override to chase anything that moves across your frame. If white balance was your Fn1 habit, it now lives one press away in the i menu.
