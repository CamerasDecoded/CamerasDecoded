Autofocus, set up right

How do you set up the R10's autofocus so it holds your subject?

https://camerasdecoded.com/camera-select/canon/r10/autofocus.html
THE ANSWER
Servo AF, Subject tracking switched on with the subject named — People, Animals or Vehicles — a Flexible Zone for control or Whole area AF for movers, and the multi-controller driving the point. Case A tunes the rest.
AF operation: Servo AF · Subject tracking: On · Subject to detect: your subject · AF area: Flexible Zone AF / Whole area AF · Case: A
Free lesson — the guided run walks every step.
STEP 1

Tracking needs Servo AF

MENU→AF→AF operation

Every tracking setup depends on Servo AF: the camera keeps adjusting while you hold the button half-pressed. One-Shot AF locks once — right for still life, wrong for anything that breathes or walks. There is no AI Focus AF on this body: no mode switches between the two for you.

STEP 2

Pick the area by the subject

MENU→AF→AF area

Spot AF and 1-point AF take one exact point. The Expand AF area modes start on your point and borrow the neighbors when the subject moves. Flexible Zone AF works a patch of the frame you can size and move — the home most shooters should live in. For movers, Whole area AF lets tracking follow across the entire frame. Aim the point with the multi-controller — Canon’s eight-way key under your thumb — or by touch and drag on the screen.

STEP 3

Switch tracking on, name the subject

MENU→AF→Subject to detect

Subject tracking must be On before any tracking frame appears — the manual says so twice. Subject to detect then names the quarry: People, Animals, Vehicles, None. There is no Auto on this body. Animals means dogs, cats and birds; Vehicles means two- or four-wheeled motorsports vehicles. Canon tunes the follow with Cases — Case A adapts both parameters automatically and is the right default.

STEP 4

Choose the eye on the screen

MENU→AF→Eye detection

With People set and Eye detection on, the tracking frame lands on an eye. Which eye is chosen live: tap it, or switch with the multi-controller. There is no Right eye/Left eye menu setting on this body — and with Subject to detect set to None, eyes are never detected.

TRY IT NOW

Set Servo AF, Subject tracking on and Whole area AF, name today's subject under Subject to detect, then track something moving across the frame and watch it follow instead of hunting.
