Which functions belong on the R7's buttons?

THE ANSWER
The multi-controller already holds Direct AF point selection — add Start/stop tracking to its press and it picks your person out of a group. M-Fn switches the AF area directly, and the AE lock button jumps straight to the eye.
Assign: Custom Functions → Customize buttons · Multi-controller: Direct AF point selection, Detail set. → Start/stop tracking · M-Fn: Direct AF area selection · AE lock: Eye Detection AF · Quick control dial: Select AF area · Save: register to C1
Pro lesson — the deep steps are in the guided run.
THE MAP

Buttons on one desk, dials on another

MENU→Custom Functions→Customize buttons

Canon splits the job: Customize buttons for the buttons, Customize dials beside it for the dials and the lens control ring. The same button can carry a different function for stills and for movies — build both sets. Multi-controller: Direct AF point selection, with Start/stop tracking on its press. M-Fn: Direct AF area selection. AE lock button: Eye Detection AF.

ASSIGNMENT 1

The stick, upgraded

Direct AF point selection is the multi-controller’s factory assignment — nudge to move the point, press straight in to center it. In Detail set., set Direct sel. on press to Start/stop tracking: with Whole area AF and Subject tracking on, one press puts the tracking frame on the person you choose out of several detected people.

ASSIGNMENT 2

M-Fn — Direct AF area selection

MENU→Custom Functions→Customize buttons

The area change is the most common mid-shoot move there is: portrait to action, Zone to Whole area. On the M-Fn button it costs one press, eye still to the viewfinder. It has to live there: Direct AF area selection cannot be assigned to the multi-controller on this body. The Quick control dial can also take Select AF area from the Customize dials desk.

ASSIGNMENT 3

AE lock — Eye Detection AF

MENU→AF→Eye detection

Eye Detection AF jumps focus straight to the subject’s eye. On this body it can only live on AF-ON, the AE lock button, the depth-of-field preview button, or a lens button — the AE lock button is its natural home. Know the other absence: there is no Direct select of subject to detect function here; picking a person is the multi-controller’s press, not a button function.

SAVE IT

The map lives in C1

MENU→Set-up→Custom shooting mode (C1-C3)→Register settings

A C1 registration captures shooting, menu and Custom Function settings — the button map included. Register the finished map to C1, and the mode dial restores your settings and your buttons together.

TRY IT NOW

Set the multi-controller’s press to Start/stop tracking in Detail set., then practice picking one person out of a group before adding the rest of the map.
