Common fixes

What fixes the R7's known problems — 4K that won't record, adapted lenses, focus that won't track?

https://camerasdecoded.com/camera-select/canon/eos-r7/common-fixes.html
THE ANSWER
4K refuses or stops: low level format the card and meet the card spec — V60 for 4K 10-bit. An adapted lens that won't fire or stabilize: let the shutter release without a lens, then set its focal length. Focus that won't follow: Subject tracking itself must be On. And breathing has no correction on this body — know it before you hunt for it.
4K: Set-up → Format card → Low level format · V60 card for 4K 10-bit · Adapted lens: Custom Functions → Release shutter w/o lens → ON + IS mode → Focal length · Tracking: AF → Subject tracking → On · Breathing: no correction item on this body

FIX 1

4K stops — or never starts

MENU→Set-up→Format card

Work the checklist in order. Format: Canon’s manual says to low level format the card before 4K recording. Card speed: 4K at 10-bit (Canon Log or HDR PQ) needs a V60 card by Canon’s requirements; 8-bit asks for UHS Speed Class 3 — a slow card stops recordings or fails them outright. Heat closes the list: a hot camera offers less recording time.

FIX 2

It won’t fire — and it won’t steady

MENU→Custom Functions→Release shutter w/o lens

A manual or adapted lens with no electronic contacts fails twice: the shutter will not fire until Release shutter w/o lens is set to ON (Custom Functions tab), and the in-body stabilizer cannot work until [Focal length] is set to the lens’s length in IS (Image Stabilizer) mode. The manual ties them: with release-without-lens disabled, stabilization is prevented.

FIX 3

No tracking frame, ever

MENU→AF→Subject tracking

Subject to detect is set, Servo is on, and no tracking frame appears: the cause is Subject tracking itself. Set it to On. The manual states it twice — with Subject tracking off, no tracking frame is displayed regardless of the Subject to detect setting, and eyes are not detected either.

FIX 4

The honest answer: it isn’t here

MENU→Shooting→Lens aberration correction

Focus breathing — the frame pulsing at the edges as focus racks — is corrected on bigger Canons by a Focus breathing correction item. This body does not have it: Lens aberration correction here lists Peripheral Illumination, Distortion, Digital Lens Optimizer, Chromatic Aberration and Diffraction only. Manage breathing with lens choice, shorter focus throws, and cuts.
