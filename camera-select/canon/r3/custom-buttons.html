Custom button map

How do you map the EOS R3's controls — the Smart Controller that moves the AF point under your thumb, M-Fn for the AF area, and Eye Control on a button?

camerasdecoded.com/camera-select/canon/r3/custom-buttons
The answer — share this
M-Fn switches the AF area directly, the AE lock button switches the subject to detect, depth-of-field preview jumps straight to Eye Detection AF, and the Smart Controller moves the AF point under your thumb — the four mid-shoot changes, under your fingers.
Assign: Custom Functions → Customize buttons  ·  Smart Controller: Direct AF pt sel (during meter)  ·  M-Fn: Direct AF area selection  ·  AE lock: Direct select of sub. to detect  ·  DOF preview: Eye Detection AF  ·  Save: register to C1
Cameras Decoded — Camera Select
Step 1

Customize buttons — and dials beside it

MENU→Custom Functions→Customize buttons

Everything lives in two places. Customize buttons assigns functions to the buttons; Customize dials, right beside it, assigns the Main dial, the Quick Control dials and the lens control ring. One rule shapes the whole map: different functions, for stills or movies, can be assigned to the same button — set both sides, or the movie side will surprise you the first time you roll. Pick a control, pick a function, press SET; functions marked with INFO have advanced settings behind the INFO button. And one housekeeping fact: Customize buttons settings are NOT cleared by Clear all Custom Func. — only Clear customized settings wipes them, so your map survives a reset.

Step 2

The thumb pad that moves the point

MENU→Custom Functions→Customize buttons→Smart controller

The Smart Controller is the AF-ON button and a touch pad in one. Press it and the camera focuses — the same as a half-press of the shutter. Drag on it and the AF point moves under your thumb, with adjustable sensitivity for how far the point travels per drag. The assignment that makes it sing: Direct AF pt sel (during meter) — with it assigned, you can move the AF point directly as long as the metering timer is running, no press needed. The vertical grip carries the same control, so portrait shooting keeps the same thumb. Two honest limits from the manual: gloves and wet fingers — the pad may not respond to either, so in rain or cold, fall back to the multi-controller joystick. If the pad ever fights you, Custom Functions → Smart controller can disable it — or just the vertical one.

Step 3

The two free moves

MENU→Custom Functions→Customize buttons→M-Fn button

M-Fn: Direct AF area selection. One press and you are cycling areas — Zone to Spot to Whole area — without taking your eye from the viewfinder. The depth-of-field preview button is the least-used control on the body; give it Eye Detection AF, and one press puts the eye in focus whatever the area. And Eye Control itself can live on a button: Move AF point by eye control confirms the subject your gaze has picked, Moving AF point, start AF by eye ctrl does the move and the focus in one press, and Eye control toggles the whole feature on and off. One more worth knowing: Register/recall shooting func stores a full shooting setup and recalls it while you hold the button — the panic button for a sudden change of light. It is unavailable while Panning Assist is on.

Step 4

The subject switch

MENU→Custom Functions→Customize buttons→AE lock button

The subject changes mid-shoot — person to dog, dog to race car — and the menu is too slow. Assign Direct select of sub. to detect to the AE lock button: one press cycles the subject — People, Animals, Vehicles — with no menu and no missed frame. Pair it with M-Fn on the area and the depth-of-field preview button on Eye Detection AF, and the three mid-shoot changes are all under your fingers at once.

Step 5

Save the map to C1

MENU→Set-up→Custom shooting mode (C1-C3)→Register settings

Register the map to C1. Custom Function settings are part of a registration — one dial twist restores the whole map, buttons, dials and all. And remember the two housekeeping facts: your button assignments survive Clear all Custom Func. (only Clear customized settings wipes them), and a firmware update deletes registered data — save the settings to the card first with Save/load cam settings on card. The vertical grip duplicates the Smart Controller and multi-controller, so the map works in both orientations without a second setup.

Try it now

Assign M-Fn only, first — Direct AF area selection — and shoot with it for a day. Add the depth-of-field preview assignment when M-Fn is muscle memory, then the Smart Controller tuning. Finish by registering the map to C1.
