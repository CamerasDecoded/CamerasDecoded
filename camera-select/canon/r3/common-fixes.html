Common fixes

What do you do when your EOS R3 will not cooperate — Eye Control that will not calibrate, a movie button that does nothing, a burst that never reaches 30 fps, or a take that stops on its own?

camerasdecoded.com/camera-select/canon/r3/common-fixes
The answer — share this
Eye Control that will not calibrate is almost always eyewear or eye position. A movie button that does nothing is the SD card rule. A burst below 30 fps is the shutter speed or flicker reduction. A take that stops is heat or a card too slow — and after an overheat stop, the camera will not shoot until it cools.
Eye Control: same eyewear as calibration, four corners even, both eyes open  ·  Movie button: RAW / HFR / 4K 60p ALL-I are CFexpress-only  ·  30 fps: Electronic + H+, 1/125 or faster, flicker reduction off  ·  Heat: red icon means stop soon — cool down before shooting again

Eye Control will not calibrateEyewear or eye position. Canon's own list of what blocks it: hard contact lenses, color contact lenses, mirrored or bifocal glasses, sunglasses, a mask — and an eye too far from, too close to, or misaligned with the viewfinder, a dirty or fogged viewfinder, or sunlight shining directly into it. The fix is a checklist: calibrate under the same eyewear conditions you shoot in (a different CAL number for glasses and for contacts), hold the camera so the four corners of the screen appear evenly spaced, keep both eyes open, avoid squinting and blinking, and repeat the calibration under different brightness levels for the same CAL number. If the pointer stops following your gaze mid-shoot, hold the camera away from your face for a moment, then look through again with the four corners even. If Eye Control throws Err50, update the firmware — Canon fixed it in version 2.0.0.
The movie button does nothingRAW, HFR and 4K 60p ALL-I are CFexpress-only. The manual states it directly: RAW movies (6K RAW), 4K 119.9/100 fps High Frame Rate movies, and 4K 59.94/50 fps ALL-I cannot be recorded to SD cards. Pressing the movie shooting button will not start recording. The fix is one of two moves: switch the recording card to Card 1, the CFexpress slot, or change the codec to one the SD card can take — 4K IPB, or Full HD. Two more card rules: individual movie files over 4 GB cannot be recorded to SD cards, and Rec. to multiple is not available for RAW movies, 4K 60p ALL-I, or 4K High Frame Rate — those go to one card only.
The burst never reaches 30 fpsShutter speed below 1/125, or flicker reduction on. 30 fps needs a shutter speed of 1/125 or faster and flicker reduction off. Check the two obvious ones too: Shutter mode must be Electronic (Mechanical and Electronic 1st-curtain top out at 12 fps) and Drive mode must be H+ (H tops out at 15). Aperture changes and zooming during the burst slow it down as well. The related failure is banding: light and dark stripes across the frame mean the electronic shutter met flickering light — fluorescent tubes, or another camera's flash. Switch the Shutter mode to Mechanical or Electronic 1st-curtain, or enable Anti-flicker shoot. or HF anti-flicker shooting.
The take stops on its ownHeat — or a card too slow for the bit rate. Watch the icons: a white icon means the camera is getting warm; a red icon means movie recording will soon terminate automatically. After an auto-stop, you cannot record movies or shoot stills until the camera has cooled down — turn it off and wait. Buy time before the shoot: set Auto Power Off Temperature to High, and remember the cards may become hot on High — wait until the card has cooled down before removing it. If heat is not the cause, the card is too slow for the bit rate — format with Low level format and use a VPG400 CFexpress card. Two more stops that look like failures: a High Frame Rate take stops automatically at 89 minutes 29 seconds, and a movie file over 4 GB cannot be recorded to an SD card.
Only one card filledRec. to multiple was never set. Out of the box the camera records to one card and the second slot sits idle. Set Rec options to Rec. to multiple under Set-up → Record func+card/folder sel. and every shot lands on both cards. The mirror-image failure: with Rec. to multiple on, Card full appears when ONE card fills and shooting stops, even if the other card has room — replace the card, or switch Rec options to Standard and pick the card with free space.
The AF point turns orangeThe camera could not focus. An orange AF point means the camera could not focus on the subject — recompose and try again. Faces cannot be detected when the subject is greatly out of focus: focus manually first to bring the face into range, then let AF take over. AF may not detect subjects or faces at the edges of the screen — center the subject or the AF point before shooting. And remember that tapping the screen always focuses with One-Shot AF, even when the camera is set to Servo AF.
Try it now

Check the firmware — current is 2.1.0 — set Auto Power Off Temperature to High, and recalibrate Eye Control under the eyewear you actually shoot in. Then shoot a burst at 30 fps and confirm the frame rate holds.
