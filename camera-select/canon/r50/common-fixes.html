Common fixes

What fixes the EOS R50's known problems — 4K that won't record, adapted lenses, focus that won't roam?

https://camerasdecoded.com/camera-select/canon/r50/common-fixes.html
THE ANSWER
4K refuses or stops: low level format the card and meet the card spec — UHS Speed Class 3 is the top ask on this UHS-I body. An adapted lens that won't fire: let the shutter release without a lens — and know this body gives silent glass no stabilization for stills. Focus that won't roam: Whole area tracking Servo AF must be On, in Servo AF. And breathing DOES have a correction here — inside Lens aberration correction, behind Distortion correction, for movies.
4K: Set-up → Format card → Low level format · UHS Speed Class 3 card for 4K · Adapted lens: Custom Functions → Release shutter w/o lens → ON · Roaming: AF → Whole area tracking Servo AF → On (Servo AF) · Breathing: Shooting → Lens aberration correction → Focus breathing correction (Distortion correction On first)
Free lesson — the guided run walks every step.
FIX 1

4K stops — or never starts

MENU→Set-up→Format card

Work the checklist in order. Format: Canon’s manual says to low level format the card before 4K recording. Card speed: 4K needs a UHS Speed Class 3 card by Canon’s requirements — the top of the scale here, since the slot is UHS-I and a UHS-II badge buys nothing. A slow card stops recordings or fails them outright. Heat closes the list: a hot camera offers less recording time.

FIX 2

It won’t fire — and it won’t steady

MENU→Custom Functions→Release shutter w/o lens

A manual or adapted lens with no electronic contacts leaves the shutter dead until Release shutter w/o lens is set to ON (Custom Functions tab). The stabilization half is honest: this body has no in-body stabilizer and no [Focal length] entry, so silent glass shoots unstabilized for stills. For movies, Movie Digital IS can stabilize even a non-IS lens.

FIX 3

Tracking stays in the point

MENU→AF→Whole area tracking Servo AF

Servo tracks — but only inside your AF points, and the subject walks out of the fence. The cause is Whole area tracking Servo AF. Set it to On, in Servo AF: On lets tracking follow subjects across the entire screen, while Off tracks only within AF points, in the manual’s own words. There is no Subject tracking switch on this body.

FIX 4

The correction is here — behind a door

MENU→Shooting→Lens aberration correction

Focus breathing — the frame pulsing at the edges as focus racks — is corrected on this body by a Focus breathing correction item inside Lens aberration correction, a list the R7 and R10 do not carry it on. Two conditions: it appears only in movie shooting, and it can be set only when Distortion correction is enabled. It slightly narrows the angle of view and depends on a compatible lens — Canon advises testing it before a real shoot.

TRY IT NOW

Rehearse the fixes now: low level format the card you shoot 4K on, switch Whole area tracking Servo AF On, and enable Focus breathing correction in movie mode to watch the pulse stop.
