Custom button map

Which functions belong on the EOS R50's buttons?

https://camerasdecoded.com/camera-select/canon/r50/custom-buttons.html
THE ANSWER
The movie button starts and stops whole-area tracking in stills mode — Canon's own pick-and-follow workflow — the lens control ring switches the AF area, and the Down key jumps to the eye. The point itself stays hardware: the AF point selection button and the cross keys, because this body has no Direct AF point selection function to assign.
Assign: Custom Functions → Customize buttons · Movie button (stills set): Start/stop whole area AF tracking · Down key: Eye detection · Control ring: Customize control ring → Select AF area
Pro lesson — the deep steps are in the guided run.
THE MAP

Buttons on one desk, the ring on another

MENU→Custom Functions→Customize buttons

Canon splits the job: Customize buttons for the buttons, Customize control ring beside it for the lens ring — there is no dials desk on a one-dial body. The same button can carry a different function for stills and for movies — build both sets. Movie button (stills set): Start/stop whole area AF tracking. Control ring: Select AF area. Down key: Eye detection.

ASSIGNMENT 1

One press picks your subject

Start/stop whole area AF tracking on the movie button (stills set) is Canon’s documented workflow: press, and the tracking frame locks onto your subject and follows it across the entire screen, regardless of the AF area in use. Press again to release. With several people detected, the cross keys walk the frame to your person.

ASSIGNMENT 2

The ring takes the area

MENU→Custom Functions→Customize control ring

Select AF area on the control ring switches areas at the lens, eye still up — the trade is the ring’s factory job, exposure compensation in the Creative Zone modes. Know the absence: there is no Direct AF point selection function on this body. The point is hardware — the AF point selection button plus the cross keys, or touch and drag.

ASSIGNMENT 3

Down key — Eye detection

MENU→AF→Eye detection

Eye detection engages eye focus with one press. It can live on the movie button, the ISO button, any cross key, or a lens button — the Down key is its natural home here, the one cross key with no shooting job of its own (its Erase label belongs to playback). The cross keys’ factory jobs are worth knowing before you reassign any of them: Left switches focus mode, Right picks the drive mode.

SAVE IT

The map stays

MENU→Custom Functions→Customize buttons

Customize buttons settings are not cleared even by Clear all Custom Func. (C.Fn) — the manual says so. Only Clear customized settings wipes them. There is no C1 registration for stills on this body, and the map does not need one: build it once and it persists.

TRY IT NOW

Set the movie button’s stills assignment to Start/stop whole area AF tracking, then practice picking one person out of a group before adding the rest of the map.
