Common fixes

What fixes the R8's known problems — 4K clips that stop early, focus breathing, the wrong subject, manual lenses?

https://camerasdecoded.com/camera-select/canon/eos-r8/common-fixes.html
THE ANSWER
4K stops early: low-level format the card, use a V60 card for 4K 10-bit, and plan around 40 minutes per LP-E17. Breathing: Focus breathing correction, with Distortion correction on first. Wrong subject: Subject to detect. Manual lens: let the shutter release without a lens.
4K: Set-up → Format card → Low level format · Breathing: Shooting → Lens aberration correction → Focus breathing correction · Subject: AF → Subject to detect · Manual lens: Custom Functions → Release shutter w/o lens → ON

4K clips stop long before the card fillsLow level format, a fast enough card, a fresh battery.

MENU → Set-up → Format card → Low level format before 4K recording. 4K 59.94 in 10-bit needs a V60 card by Canon’s requirements, and one LP-E17 is good for roughly 40 minutes of 4K 59.94 — most stopped clips are the battery. A hot camera offers less time.

Frame pulses when focus racksFocus breathing correction.

MENU → Shooting → Lens aberration correction. The item appears only when Distortion correction is set to Enable, and it is a movie-only correction that narrows the angle of view slightly.

Focus locks the wrong subjectCheck Subject to detect.

MENU → AF → Subject to detect. A subject left over from the last shoot is the usual cause. For portraits, set Eye detection to the near eye.

Manual lens: dead shutterRelease shutter w/o lens → ON.

Custom Functions → Release shutter w/o lens → ON, so the shutter fires with a contactless lens. This body has no stabilizer of its own and no focal-length entry — a manual lens without IS shoots unstabilized.

TRY IT NOW

Low level format your card now (after offloading it), then open Subject to detect and confirm it names what you're shooting today — not yesterday.
