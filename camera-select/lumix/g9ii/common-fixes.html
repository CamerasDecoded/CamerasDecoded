Common fixes

Why won't your Panasonic Lumix G9 II record that quality, why did the take stop, why does V-Log look flat, and why won't the shutter fire? The four fixes, from the manual.

camerasdecoded.com/camera-select/lumix/g9ii/common-fixes
The answer — share this
The four classics, all in the manual: a quality that won't record is one no SD card can take — MOV at 800 Mbps, ProRes above FHD, or ALL-Intra variable frame rate all need an external SSD; a flat, dark monitor on V-Log is log without its View Assist LUT; a shutter that won't fire is Focus/Shutter Priority on FOCUS, waiting for focus that never comes; and a take that stops is heat doing its stated job — about 30 minutes of 4K from a cold start, on a body with no fan.
Cards: MOV 800 Mbps / ProRes above FHD / ALL-Intra VFR = external SSD only  ·  Heat: Setup (Monitor / Display) → Thermal Management  ·  Preview: V-Log View Assist → LUT View Assist (Monitor)

The quality won't record, or is greyed outNo SD card can take it. MOV video at 800 Mbps or more, Apple ProRes video above FHD, and variable frame rate video with an ALL-Intra recording quality cannot be recorded to SD cards — the manual's card chapter states it outright. Record those qualities to an external SSD on the USB-C port (up to 2 TB, USB 3.2 Gen 2), with Setup (Card/File) → USB-SSD set to ON. The same chapter warns that a slow SD card can stop a recording mid-take: match the speed class to the bit rate (V90 for up to 600 Mbps, V60 for up to 400 Mbps, V30 for up to 200 Mbps).
V-Log looks flat and dark on the monitorThat is log without its preview. The manual notes the recording screen darkens with V-Log — the contrast is being saved for grading. Custom (Monitor / Display (Video)) → V-Log View Assist: choose the LUT (Vlog_709 or a LUT Library file) under LUT Select and turn LUT View Assist (Monitor) — and, for an external screen, LUT View Assist (HDMI) — on. Both switches ship OFF. The file stays flat; only the preview is graded. If you do not grade, shoot REAL TIME LUT instead and record the look directly. View Assist is unavailable when a LUT is already baked into the Photo Style.
The shutter won't fireThe camera is waiting for focus. With Focus/Shutter Priority on FOCUS (the AFS default), the manual states recording will not take place until focus is achieved. The troubleshooting list adds: Shutter AF set to OFF (the half-press never focuses), Focus Limiter ON (the subject is fenced out of range), AF Lock holding an old point, and AF Micro Adjustment set wrong. Aim at something with contrast, work the list, or set the priority to BALANCE.
The take stopped by itselfHeat, with a warning first. The manual says the camera rises in temperature fastest in hot surroundings, during continuous high-definition recording, and while outputting via HDMI — and this body has no cooling fan. From a cold start at 23 °C, the guide is about 30 minutes at C4K/4K and about 15 minutes at C4K/4K 120p (about 7 and 3 minutes at 40 °C). A temperature icon blinks first, then a message appears and recording and HDMI output stop. Wait for the camera to cool until the message says it can be used again, then turn it off and on. For long takes set Setup (Monitor / Display) → Thermal Management → Recording Max Temperature to HIGH — and use a tripod: at HIGH the body gets hot, and Panasonic warns of low-temperature burns from prolonged contact.
Try it now

Confirm both SD cards are formatted, connect the SSD if the take needs it, read the Thermal Management screen, then toggle LUT View Assist on a V-Log scene. Finish by pressing the shutter with the lens cap on — feel the FOCUS priority refuse, then switch to BALANCE.
