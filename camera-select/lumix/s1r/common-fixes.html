Common Fixes

What do you do when the Panasonic Lumix S1R will not fire, will not focus, stops recording, or will not turn Silent Mode off?

https://camerasdecoded.com/camera-select/lumix/s1r/common-fixes.html
THE ANSWER
A shutter that will not fire is Focus/Shutter Priority on FOCUS; Silent Mode that will not turn off is the Fn lever in MODE2; video that stops mid-take is a slow SD card or the 15-minute 4K cap; a flash that will not fire is the electronic shutter, Silent Mode, or High Resolution Mode.
Shutter: Photo (Focus) → Focus/Shutter Priority · Silent: Fn lever → MODE1 · Video: SD card V30+; 4K cap 15 min · Flash: Shutter Type MECH. or EFC · Bright spot: Setup (Others) → Pixel Refresh · Last resort: Setup (Setting) → Reset

STEP 1

The shutter will not fire

MENU→Photo Menu (Focus)→Focus/Shutter Priority

If the shutter button does nothing, check Focus/Shutter Priority first: on FOCUS, the camera refuses to shoot until focus locks — and with a low-contrast subject in dim light, that lock may never come. Switch it to RELEASE and the shutter fires on command. The other usual causes: Shutter AF set to OFF, so the half-press never focuses at all, or AF Lock set where it should not be. And check the subject is inside the lens’s focus range — the 24-105mm kit lens focuses no closer than 0.30 m.

STEP 2

Silent Mode will not turn off

MENU→Custom Menu (Operation)→Fn Lever Setting

The Fn lever next to the lens ships registered to Silent Mode: MODE1 is normal, MODE2 is silent. While the lever sits in MODE2, the menu cannot turn Silent Mode off — flip the lever to MODE1. Silent Mode matters beyond sound: it fixes the shutter to ELEC., and the electronic shutter cannot fire a flash and distorts moving subjects. If the flash suddenly stopped working and the shutter went quiet at the same time, this is almost certainly why.

STEP 3

Video stops in the middle of a take

MENU→Video Menu (Image Format)→Rec Quality

Two causes, and the fix depends on which one you hit. If the take stops at 15 minutes in 4K, that is the manual’s cap — every 4K mode stops there, and Full HD is the format for long takes. If it stops early and at random, the SD card is too slow: 4K video needs UHS Speed Class 3, V30 or higher, and a slower card stops the recording mid-take. The third cause is heat: if the camera overheats, it stops recording and locks out video, 6K/4K Photo, Post-Focus, and HDMI output until it cools down.

STEP 4

The flash will not fire

MENU→Photo Menu (Others (Photo))→Shutter Type

First, the S1R has no built-in flash — an external flash is the only kind. If the external flash will not fire, the cause is almost always a mode that forbids it: the electronic shutter (ELEC.) cannot fire a flash, Silent Mode fixes the shutter to ELEC., and the flash does not fire in video, 6K/4K Photo, Post-Focus, High Resolution Mode, or with Filter Settings. Set Shutter Type to MECH. or EFC and try again. And mind the sync speed: 1/320 is the fastest shutter that syncs, and the guide number drops at 1/320 in S and M modes.

TRY IT NOW

Set Focus/Shutter Priority to FOCUS and try to shoot a blank wall — feel the refusal. Flip the Fn lever to MODE2, then back. Set Shutter Type to ELEC. and watch the flash option disappear.
