Autofocus

How do you set up autofocus on the Panasonic Lumix S1R — areas, subject detection, and the tracking settings that actually hold?

https://camerasdecoded.com/camera-select/lumix/s1r/autofocus.html
THE ANSWER
Set the lever to C for motion, pick Human/Animal Detect AF to let the camera find faces, eyes, and animals, tune tracking with AF Custom Setting Sets 1–4, and remember it is contrast AF — Pinpoint never works in AFC, and AFC quietly becomes AFS in low light.
Lever: S / C / MF · Area: Photo (Focus) → AF Mode · Detection: Human/Animal Detect AF (animals win over humans) · Tracking: Photo (Focus) → AF Custom Setting(Photo) · Override: AF+MF
Free lesson — the guided run walks every step.
STEP 1

Pick the area — 225 down to Pinpoint

MENU→Photo Menu (Focus)→AF Mode

AF Mode picks the area, and the range is wide: 225-Area covers the frame, Zone narrows it to a vertical/horizontal band, square, or oval — Square and Oval are off by default, enable them in Show/Hide AF Mode — 1-Area+ adds a supplementary area that catches the subject if it leaves the box, 1-Area is one precise box, and Pinpoint magnifies for exact placement. Two limits to learn now: Pinpoint is not available in AFC at all, and Tracking only tracks in AFC — in AFS it aligns once and stops.

STEP 2

Let it find the subject

MENU→Photo Menu (Focus)→AF Mode

Human/Animal Detect AF is the current name — firmware 1.6 renamed it from Face/Eye/Body/Animal Detect, and added head detection, so the camera now finds a person whose face is turned away. It detects faces, eyes, and bodies, plus birds, canines, and felines — and animals are prioritized over humans when both are in the frame. Up to 15 faces, up to 3 bodies at once. The same update brought auto detection into 1-Area and 1-Area+: when a person or animal enters your chosen box, the detection frame turns yellow. If nothing is detected, the camera falls back to 225-Area.

STEP 3

Tune the tracking

MENU→Photo Menu (Focus)→AF Custom Setting(Photo)

AF Custom Setting(Photo) holds four tracking recipes. Set 1 is the general setting. Set 2 suits a subject moving in one direction at constant speed. Set 3 handles random movement and obstacles crossing the frame. Set 4 follows subjects whose speed changes. Pick the set that matches the shoot instead of leaving Set 1 for everything — a dog running toward you and a cyclist crossing the frame are not the same tracking problem.

STEP 4

The honest limit: contrast AF

MENU→Photo Menu (Focus)→AF+MF

This camera focuses by contrast detection — DFD, depth from defocus — with no phase-detection pixels on the sensor. Know what it struggles with: fast-moving subjects, subjects without contrast, subjects through windows, subjects near shiny objects, and very dark locations. AFC also quietly becomes AFS in low light, in the 65:24 and 2:1 aspect ratios, and in High Resolution Mode. The escape hatch is AF+MF: with it on, turning the focus ring during AFC hands you manual focus, and letting go hands it back. Low illumination AF and Starlight AF help at night.

TRY IT NOW

Set the lever to C, turn on Human/Animal Detect AF, pick AF Custom Setting Set 3, and track something moving — then turn the focus ring mid-track and feel AF+MF take over.
