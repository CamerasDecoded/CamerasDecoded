Video Settings

How do you set up video on the Panasonic Lumix S1R — frame rates, codecs, and profiles, and what are its real limits?

https://camerasdecoded.com/camera-select/lumix/s1r/video-settings.html
THE ANSWER
4K tops out at 60p with a 1.09x crop and a 15-minute cap on every 4K mode; the 10-bit path is MOV with 5K at 200 Mbps; HLG comes from Like2100(HLG) in Creative Video mode — and there is no V-Log on this camera at any price.
Quality: Video (Image Format) → Rec Quality · 4K: 1.09x crop, 15-min cap · Format: Video (Image Format) → Rec. File Format → MOV · 5K: 4992×3744, 10-bit, 200 Mbps · HLG: Video (Image Quality) → Photo Style → Like2100(HLG) · V-Log: none — do not buy DMW-SFU2
Pro lesson — the deep steps are in the guided run.
STEP 1

The frame: 4K is cropped and capped

MENU→Video Menu (Image Format)→Rec Quality

4K on this camera tops out at 60p: 4K60p/50p at 150 Mbps, 4K30p/25p/24p at 100 Mbps, all 4:2:0 8-bit. Two limits define it. First, every 4K mode carries a 1.09x crop — a slight narrowing of the full frame, at every frame rate. Second, every 4K mode stops when continuous recording exceeds 15 minutes. Full HD has neither limit. Image Area of Video offers FULL, APS-C, or PIXEL/PIXEL; the APS-C area is the sharpest 4K this camera makes, and High Speed Video at 180/150p fixes the area to APS-C.

STEP 2

The 10-bit path: MOV and 5K

MENU→Video Menu (Image Format)→Rec. File Format

Firmware 1.6 added MOV to Rec. File Format, and MOV unlocks the best video this camera records: 5K at 30p, 25p, or 24p — 4992×3744, 4:2:0 10-bit, 200 Mbps LongGOP with LPCM audio. The trade-offs: the image area is fixed to PIXEL/PIXEL, so the angle of view narrows; the 15-minute cap applies; E-Stabilization is not available in 5K; APS-C lenses cannot record 5K at all; and the SD card needs UHS Speed Class 3 or higher. A 64 GB card holds about 42 minutes of 5K.

STEP 3

The HDR path: Like2100(HLG) — and no V-Log

MENU→Video Menu (Image Quality)→Photo Style

Firmware 1.6 also added Like2100(HLG) to Photo Style — HLG video with a wide dynamic range, selectable only in Creative Video mode with a MOV 10-bit recording quality. With it on, the ISO floor rises to 320. The monitor and viewfinder do not display HLG; HLG View Assist converts the image for checking. And the fact that saves you money: there is no V-Log on the S1R — not built in, and no upgrade key exists. The DMW-SFU2 key is for the S1 only. Do not buy it for this camera.

STEP 4

Sound, shutter angle, and the red frame

MENU→Video Menu (Audio)→Sound Rec Level Adj.

Sound Rec Level Adj. runs from −12 dB to +6 dB; the XLR Mic Adaptor DMW-XLR1 records 96 kHz/24-bit audio, but only in MOV format. SS/Gain Operation, from firmware 1.6, lets video shooters think in shutter angle — 11° to 358° — or gain in dB, where 0 dB equals ISO 100. Turn on the Red REC Frame Indicator and a red frame marks the recording screen while rolling. And over HDMI, the camera outputs 4K60p/50p in 4:2:2 10-bit to an external recorder — but while it does, nothing is recorded to the card.

TRY IT NOW

Set Rec. File Format to MOV, pick 5K/10bit/200M/24p, set Photo Style to Like2100(HLG), and turn on the Red REC Frame Indicator — then watch the ISO floor rise to 320.
