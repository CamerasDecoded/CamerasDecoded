Custom Buttons

Which buttons should you reassign on the Panasonic Lumix S1R — and what does the Fn lever actually do?

https://camerasdecoded.com/camera-select/lumix/s1r/custom-buttons.html
THE ANSWER
Fn1 ships as AF-Point Scope and Fn2 as Preview, but Fn3 and Fn6–Fn16 ship empty — press and hold any Fn button for two seconds to reassign it, remember the Fn lever is a Silent Mode switch until you change it, and save the finished map to C1.
Fn: Custom (Operation) → Fn Button Set · Reassign: press and hold 2 sec · Lever: Custom (Operation) → Fn Lever Setting · Save: Setup (Setting) → Save to Custom Mode
Pro lesson — the deep steps are in the guided run.
STEP 1

The factory map — and the two-second trick

MENU→Custom Menu (Operation)→Fn Button Set

This body has sixteen Fn buttons in recording mode, and most of them ship empty. The factory map: Fn1 on the front is AF-Point Scope — press and hold to magnify the focus point — Fn2 is Preview, Fn4 is Histogram, Fn5 is Boost I.S. (Video). Fn3 and Fn6 through Fn16 are No Setting. Fn3 to Fn7 are Touch Tab icons, and the Touch Tab itself is off by default. The fast way to change any of them: press and hold the Fn button for two seconds and its registration screen opens.

STEP 2

The Fn lever is a Silent Mode switch — until you change it

MENU→Custom Menu (Operation)→Fn Lever Setting

The Fn lever next to the lens ships as a Silent Mode switch: MODE1 is normal, MODE2 is silent. That is also the most common trap on this camera — Silent Mode will not turn off from the menu while the lever sits in MODE2; flip the lever back to MODE1. The lever can hold one other function instead, and one function lives here only: the Front/Rear/Control Dial Lock can only be set via the Fn lever. Set the lever’s job at Fn Lever Setting.

STEP 3

The assignments that earn a button

MENU→Custom Menu (Operation)→Fn Button Set

First, the two that fix focus mistakes: AF-ON Near Shift and AF-ON Far Shift, added in firmware 1.2 — Near Shift prioritizes close subjects when the camera keeps grabbing the background, Far Shift prioritizes distant subjects when you shoot through fences or nets. Then the working roles: Destination Card Slot, Shutter Type, Image Stabilizer, E-Stabilization (Video), and Photo Style — including L.ClassicNeo and L.Monochrome S from firmware 1.9. Put the ones you reach for mid-shoot on the buttons your fingers already find: Fn1, Fn2, and the joystick center, Fn8.

STEP 4

Bank the map to C1 — and the Q menu

MENU→Setup Menu (Setting)→Save to Custom Mode

Fn assignments are part of the saved setup, so bank the finished map: Save to Custom Mode stores it with everything else to C1, C2, or C3. While you are here, set up the Q menu too — Item Customize (Photo) and Item Customize (Video) choose what the Q button shows — and My Menu, which holds up to 23 of your most-used items on one page. Save the camera’s whole configuration to a card as well: Save/Restore Camera Setting keeps up to 10 copies, same model only.

TRY IT NOW

Press and hold Fn1 for two seconds, assign AF-ON Near Shift to a free button, flip the Fn lever and watch Silent Mode follow it, then save the map to C1.
