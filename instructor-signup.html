Curriculum + Script

You do not have to write a course. The entire Cameras Decoded curriculum is yours to teach from.

It comes with a complete, word-for-word teaching script and presenter notes: you see the script, your students see the guide on a screen and follow along on their phones in real time. Teach it word for word, or make it your own — use all of the curriculum or some of it, as you see fit. Your class, your call.

What you must pass

Nobody teaches under the Cameras Decoded name without passing a CD exam. Exams are matched to what you want to teach — you certify for your scope, not for everything.
