Sony A7IV Real Estate Indoor Artificial Settings

Exact settings for Sony A7IV real estate photography in indoor artificial light.

The Key Setting

Real estate photography has one problem that no single exposure solves — the window. Every interior shot contains windows that blow out to pure white when the interior is correctly exposed, or an interior that goes completely dark when the window is correctly exposed. The operator solution is exposure bracketing. Go to the camera menu, find Bracket Settings, set to three frames at two stop increments — one frame at correct interior exposure, one two stops overexposed, one two stops underexposed. The A7IV shoots all three frames automatically in a single burst. In post processing blend the correctly exposed interior with the correctly exposed window from the bracketed frames. This is not an advanced technique — it is the standard real estate photography workflow and the A7IV's bracketing system makes it completely accessible to any operator who knows it exists. Enable it before every interior shot. Additionally set White Balance to a specific Kelvin value — 3800K-4200K — rather than Auto. Auto White Balance shifts between frames in a bracketed sequence producing inconsistent color across the three exposures that makes blending difficult in post. A fixed Kelvin value produces consistent color across all three frames and makes the blending workflow straightforward.
