The Key Setting

The Sony A6700's compact size and fully articulating screen make it an excellent real estate camera for awkward angles. You can hold it low to the floor to capture a kitchen island with the golden light streaming across the tiles, above your head to capture a ceiling detail with warm window reflections, or in a tight corner where a larger full‑frame body simply wouldn't fit. The articulating screen means you can see the composition clearly from any angle—no guesswork, no contortion.
The APS‑C sensor requires wider lenses to achieve the same field of view as full‑frame. A 10‑20mm lens on the A6700 provides a 15‑30mm full‑frame equivalent—ideal for real estate interiors. Use a tripod for critical shots. The A6700's 26‑megapixel sensor provides good detail, but its dynamic range is less forgiving than full‑frame competitors. For high‑dynamic‑range golden hour interiors—rooms with bright windows and darker corners—bracket your exposures (3 shots, ±1.5 EV) and blend later. The A6700's DRO/Auto HDR setting can handle moderate dynamic range scenes, but for critical listing photos, manual bracketing produces better results.
Enable the Grid Line (Rule of Thirds) to keep verticals straight and compositions balanced. The A6700's compact size and light weight make it ideal for real estate photographers who need to move quickly through a property, shooting multiple angles without the fatigue of a heavy full‑frame kit. It is also an excellent backup body for a professional real estate photographer whose primary camera is a full‑frame Sony.
