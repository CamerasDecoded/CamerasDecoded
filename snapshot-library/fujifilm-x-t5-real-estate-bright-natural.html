The Key Setting

The X‑T5's 40MP sensor is a genuine advantage for real estate photography. The extra resolution allows for cropping and perspective correction without losing detail. Use the "Electronic Level" in the viewfinder (assignable to a function button) to ensure perfectly straight verticals and horizontals. Enable "Natural Live View" to preview the exposure as the final image will appear, which helps you balance bright windows against darker interiors without chimping. For high‑dynamic‑range rooms (bright windows, dark corners), shoot RAW and bracket exposures (3 shots, ±1.5 EV). The X‑T5's sensor handles shadow recovery exceptionally well.
