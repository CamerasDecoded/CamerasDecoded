The Key Setting

The Sony A6700’s compact size is a genuine social advantage at golden hour events. Guests are less intimidated by a small camera, and the photographer blends into the gathering rather than standing out as “the professional.” The AI‑powered autofocus inherited from the A7R V provides Human Pose Estimation—the camera predicts where the subject’s head and eyes will be, even when the face is partially obscured by warm flare or turned away. In the fluid environment of a golden hour event, this AI tracking allows you to focus on moments and composition while the camera handles the technical burden of keeping faces sharp.
Use Aperture Priority with Auto ISO; the A6700’s metering is reliable in good light. Set Subject Detection to Human, Face/Eye Priority in AF to On. For backlit scenes, enable Spot Metering linked to the focus point to expose for the subject’s face while the golden background blooms naturally. The A6700’s fully articulating screen allows you to shoot from high or low angles without contorting your body—useful for capturing children at an event or shooting over a crowd.
The A6700 is not a professional event body. Its 11 fps mechanical burst is adequate but not class‑leading, and the buffer fills more quickly than full‑frame competitors when shooting RAW. For golden hour events, shoot JPEG (Fine) or compressed RAW to extend the buffer. The camera’s strength is its combination of compact size, AI tracking, and the Creative Look profiles—use FL (Film Look) for a cinematic golden hour rendering that prevents skin tones from becoming overly orange while preserving warmth.
