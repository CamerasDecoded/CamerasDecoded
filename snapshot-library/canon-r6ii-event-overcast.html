The Key Setting

The Canon R6 Mark II in overcast event conditions has one specific advantage that no other camera in this database offers for outdoor events — the ability to shoot completely silent at 40 frames per second with full dual pixel AF II tracking in bright overcast light without any of the electronic shutter limitations that affect indoor shooting. In indoor artificial lighting environments electronic shutter produces banding under certain light sources. In outdoor overcast conditions no artificial light source is involved — the electronic shutter operates with complete reliability at any shutter speed without banding risk. This means overcast outdoor events are the ideal environment to activate High Speed Continuous Plus — the R6 Mark II's 40 frames per second electronic shutter burst mode. Go to drive mode, select High Speed Continuous Plus, confirm electronic shutter is enabled under shooting menu shutter type. At 40 frames per second across a two second burst of a first dance, a cake cutting, a group laugh, or a candid reaction the peak expression exists somewhere in those 80 frames regardless of how unpredictable the moment is. No other camera in this database offers this capability in outdoor overcast conditions with full autofocus reliability. Additionally the R6 Mark II's IBIS combined with the overcast bright ambient light means handholding at 1/250s produces sharp results even during continuous movement sequences — the stabilization system compensates for camera shake during the burst without impacting the subject freezing sharpness that the 1/250s shutter floor provides.
