The Key Setting

The X‑T5's face/eye detection has improved significantly with firmware updates, but in crowded scenes it can occasionally jump between faces. Set "AF Mode" to "Zone" and place the zone over your primary subject area. The camera will prioritize faces within that zone, reducing the chance of it locking onto a background person. This is faster than switching to Single Point and more reliable than Wide/Tracking in event chaos.
