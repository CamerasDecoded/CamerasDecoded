The Key Setting

The A7IV's 10fps mechanical burst is adequate for most sports but is not class‑leading. Its real sports strength is the AF system inherited from the A1/A7R V lineage — the tracking is sticky and reliable. Set "AF Track Sens" to 2 (responsive) for sports where subjects change speed or direction frequently. Use "AF Area" set to "Tracking: Expand Spot" — place the initial focus point on your subject, and the camera will track them across the frame. For sports where obstacles cross between you and the subject (e.g., football, soccer), set "AF Track Sens" to 1 (locked on) so the AF doesn't jump to a passing player.
