The Key Setting

The A7IV's 33MP sensor provides a meaningful resolution advantage over 24MP bodies for real estate — more detail in textures, fixtures, and views, plus more latitude for perspective correction in post. Use the "Electronic Level" in the viewfinder (assign to a custom button) to keep verticals perfectly straight. Enable "Focus Magnifier" to check critical focus on distant architectural details. For rooms with bright windows and darker interiors, use the A7IV's "DRO/Auto HDR" setting — "DRO Lv5" will automatically lift shadows while preserving highlights, giving a balanced JPEG straight out of camera. For maximum quality, bracket manually and blend later.
