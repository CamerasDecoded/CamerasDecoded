The Key Setting

The A6700's compact size is a social advantage at events. Guests are less intimidated by a small camera, and you can move through crowds without the "professional photographer" barrier. Use the "Touch Tracking" feature on the rear LCD — tap the subject on the screen and the AI tracking will follow them. This is faster than moving a focus point with the joystick in fluid event situations. Enable "Silent Shooting" for ceremonies or quiet moments; the A6700's electronic shutter is fast enough to minimize rolling shutter in most event scenarios.
