Exact settings for Nikon Z6 III portrait photography in indoor artificial light.

The Key Setting

The Nikon Z6 III's Portrait AF mode goes beyond face and eye detection into full body subject recognition. Most portrait photographers enable face detection and stop there. The Z6 III can be set to track the entire human form — shoulders, torso, pose — meaning it maintains subject lock during movement sequences, looking-away moments, and profile shots where the eye is not visible to the camera. Go into the subject detection settings, enable Portrait AF, and set subject detection sensitivity to five out of five for indoor portrait work where the background is static and the camera can confidently distinguish your subject from the environment. This setting transforms candid portrait moments — the laugh, the look away, the head turn — from focus misses into sharp captures.
