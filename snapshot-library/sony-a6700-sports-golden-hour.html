The Key Setting

The Sony A6700’s AI‑powered autofocus system—inherited from the flagship A7R V—is the camera’s greatest strength for golden hour sports. The Human Pose Estimation feature means the camera recognizes body position and predicts where the athlete will move next, maintaining focus even when the subject’s face is turned away or partially obscured by warm golden flare. This AI tracking is genuinely more advanced than the AF systems in some full‑frame competitors, and it makes the A6700 a surprisingly capable sports camera in good light.
However, the A6700 is not a professional sports body. Its 11 fps mechanical burst is adequate but not class‑leading, and the buffer fills more quickly than full‑frame competitors when shooting RAW. For golden hour sports, shoot JPEG (Fine) or Compressed RAW to extend the buffer. The camera’s compact size and light weight make it ideal for sports where you need to move quickly or shoot from awkward positions—sideline soccer, track and field, youth sports. The fully articulating screen allows you to shoot from ground level or overhead without contorting your body.
Use Subject Detection set to Human, Face/Eye Priority in AF to On, and AF Track Sens set to 2 (Responsive). The AI tracking will handle the movement; your job is to anticipate the peak action. The golden hour light is generous; position yourself with the sun at your back or at 45 degrees to illuminate athletes’ faces with warm, directional light that adds drama and depth to action images.
