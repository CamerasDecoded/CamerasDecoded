The Key Setting

Use the R6 II's "Focus Bracketing" feature for rooms where you need both the foreground (e.g., a kitchen island) and the distant view (e.g., a window to the garden) in sharp focus. The camera will automatically shoot a series of images at slightly different focus distances, which you can later blend in Canon's Digital Photo Professional software or Lightroom for a perfectly sharp interior. Also, enable "Electronic Level" in the viewfinder to avoid tilted real‑estate shots — tilted rooms are the #1 giveaway of an amateur listing photo.
