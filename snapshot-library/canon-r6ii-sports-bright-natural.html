Canon R6 Mark II Sports Bright Natural Settings

Exact settings for Canon R6 Mark II sports photography in bright natural light.
Recommended ModeShutter Priority Shutter Speed1/1000s Aperturef/2.8–f/5.6 ISOAuto, cap at 1600 Focus ModeContinuous AF Drive ModeContinuous High White BalanceDaylight

The Key Setting

Pre‑shooting (Raw Burst Mode) to capture frames from 0.5 seconds before you fully press the shutter. In sports, the decisive moment often happens before your reaction. This setting alone can save a missed goal, a dive, or a finish‑line lean. Set "AF Case" to 2 (Continue to Track Subjects, ignoring obstacles) for sports where other players cross in front of your subject.
