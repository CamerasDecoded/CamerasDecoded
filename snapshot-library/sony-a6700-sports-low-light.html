The Key Setting

The Sony A6700 in low light sports requires the same honest assessment as the Fujifilm X-T5 — the APS-C sensor has a genuine ISO ceiling of 6400 for client-deliverable sports work and at 1/500s shutter speed with f2.0 aperture that ceiling is reached quickly in dim gymnasium and indoor arena environments. The A6700 has one specific low light sports advantage the X-T5 does not — Sony's AI subject recognition system maintains tracking reliability in low light conditions that Fujifilm's subject detection cannot match because Sony's AI processing compensates for reduced contrast more effectively at the phase detection level. But tracking reliability does not solve the ISO ceiling problem. The most effective low light sports technique for the A6700 is high speed sync flash. A Sony HVL-F60RM2 speedlight mounted on the hotshoe set to high speed sync mode allows flash at shutter speeds above the standard sync speed of 1/250s — up to 1/2000s or faster. At 1/1000s with flash the effective ISO drops to 400-800 even in a completely dark gymnasium producing clean sharp sports images that no available light technique on this sensor can match. Go to the speedlight menu enable HSS high speed sync and set flash exposure compensation to minus one stop to prevent blown highlights on subjects at close range. The A6700 with HSS flash in low light sports is not a compromise — it is a different and in some scenarios superior approach to low light sports photography that produces results unavailable to available light only shooters regardless of their camera system.
