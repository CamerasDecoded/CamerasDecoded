The Key Setting

The Z6 III's partially stacked sensor provides noticeably faster sensor readout than the Z6 II, meaning less rolling shutter and more responsive AF. For portraits, enable "Portrait Impression Balance" in the Photo Shooting Menu — this subtly optimizes skin tones and contrast for human subjects. Set "AF Subject Detection Options" to "People" and "Eye Detection" to "On." The Z6 III's 3D Tracking can be activated by pressing "OK" while in AF‑C; the camera will track whatever subject is under the focus point, making it easy to lock onto a portrait subject and recompose without losing focus.
