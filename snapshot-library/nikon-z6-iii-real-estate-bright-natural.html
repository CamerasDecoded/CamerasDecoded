The Key Setting

The Z6 III's in‑body image stabilization (IBIS) is rated to 8 stops, meaning you can handhold at surprisingly slow shutter speeds. However, for real estate, a tripod is still recommended because it forces you to slow down, compose deliberately, and ensure perfect vertical alignment. Use the "Electronic Level" in the viewfinder (assign to Fn2). Enable "Focus Shift Shooting" for rooms where you need front‑to‑back sharpness — the camera will automatically capture a series of images at incrementally adjusted focus distances, which you can later stack in Nikon's NX Studio or third‑party software. This is useful for large rooms with foreground interest (e.g., a dining table) and distant windows.
