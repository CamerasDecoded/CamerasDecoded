The Key Setting

The A6700's APS-C crop factor creates the same real estate wide angle challenge as the Fujifilm X-T5 — a 16mm lens produces a full frame equivalent of 24mm, the absolute minimum usable focal length for interior real estate work. Sony's E-mount ecosystem offers two solutions depending on budget and shooting frequency. The Sigma 10-18mm f2.8 DC DN Contemporary produces a full frame equivalent of 15-27mm — genuinely wide enough for even small interior spaces — at a price point significantly below native Sony wide zoom alternatives. The Sony E 10-20mm f4 G PZ produces a full frame equivalent of 15-30mm with a power zoom mechanism that enables smooth focal length transitions during video walkthroughs — a meaningful advantage for real estate operators who shoot both stills and video listings. For operators who shoot real estate as a primary specialty the Sony E 10-20mm f4 G PZ's combined stills and video capability makes it the definitive A6700 real estate lens. For operators who shoot real estate occasionally alongside other work the Sigma 10-18mm f2.8 DC DN provides maximum wide coverage and a fast aperture that serves multiple scenarios beyond real estate interiors. Additionally enable the A6700's Sweep Panorama mode for rooms where even 10mm cannot capture the full space in a single frame. Go to shooting mode dial, select Scene, find Sweep Panorama, and pan the camera slowly across the room. The A6700 automatically stitches the panorama in-camera and delivers a single wide image that conveys room scale beyond what any fixed focal length achieves. This is not a replacement for proper wide angle real estate photography but it is a genuinely useful tool for particularly small rooms where the crop factor limitation cannot be fully overcome with available lenses.
