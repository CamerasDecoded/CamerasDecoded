The Key Setting

The Canon R6 Mark II has an in-camera HDR shooting mode that automatically brackets and blends exposures into a single finished image without requiring any post processing software. Go to shooting menu, HDR shooting, enable HDR PQ. The camera shoots three frames automatically and delivers a single HDR image that retains window detail and interior detail simultaneously. For real estate agents shooting their own listings who have no Lightroom or Photoshop workflow this single feature produces professional quality interior images straight from the camera with zero post processing required. Additionally set White Balance to a fixed Kelvin value between 3800K and 4200K before beginning any interior session. The R6 Mark II's Auto White Balance shifts between HDR frames during the bracketing sequence producing color inconsistency across the blended image. A fixed Kelvin value locks color consistency across all frames and produces a clean final result. Go to White Balance, select Kelvin, and set 4000K as your starting point for mixed indoor artificial light. Adjust warmer toward 4500K for tungsten dominant spaces and cooler toward 3500K for LED dominant spaces.
