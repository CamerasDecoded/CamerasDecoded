// What it does

Briefed, not connected

The skill is a plain-text file. An assistant that has it can answer its user about Cameras Decoded correctly — and hand them a link that lands in the right place.

Tonight's challenge

Reads tonight.json, checks the date, and tells the user what tonight's Arena challenge is — never invented, never yesterday's.

The right lesson

Points to the Field Manual chapter or Protocol that teaches what the user asked about, with a link. It does not rewrite the lesson from memory.

The right link

Deep links into the app: tonight's Arena, a protocol, the free page, signup landing in Chapter 1.

Plain pricing

Free to start, Pro at $119 a year, the $47 guide. It links to product pages, never to a checkout link.

// What it never does

Read-only, on purpose

This is rung one of agent access. The skill can read and explain. It cannot act — and that is the contract, not a gap.

—Never enters a photo in the Arena for the user. Entering happens in the app, by the member, with a real photo they shot.
—Never votes. Blind judging only counts when a human looked and chose.
—Never buys anything. It links to product pages; checkout happens with the user.
—Never presents an AI-generated image as a photograph. In the Arena, only real photos count.
—Never invents tonight's challenge. It reads tonight.json or says the challenge is unknown.
