File // HIRE-02 — The bar

What "vetted" actually means.

The word is worthless without a checkable bar. Here's ours — published, enforced, no exceptions.

Tested skill, not self-reported

Passed our proctored shooting exam and client-scenario assessments — pricing, rights, and the difficult-client conversation — before touching a booking.

Portfolio reviewed

Twenty images scored against a rubric: exposure control, composition, consistency across a set, editing restraint. Reviewed by a working professional.

Trial gig completed

Every photographer shoots a supervised trial before any solo booking. Nobody's first real gig is your wedding.

Rated after every job

Clients rate every shoot. Fall below 4.5 stars and the badge comes down. One no-show and they're out — no second chances on reliability.

ID-verified and contracted

Government ID on file, signed contractor agreement, real accountability. And on your side: every client is verified too — ID and card on file before we dispatch anyone. Our photographers never chase payment.

File // HIRE-08 — Straight answers

Asked, answered.

What if my photographer cancels?

We rematch you free, or refund your deposit in full. Your date is covered one way or another.

Do I get the RAW files?

Only if it's in the brief. Standard delivery is a finished, edited gallery — the photographer’s craft is part of what you’re buying.

How fast do I get my photos?

Turnaround is set in the brief before booking. Typical: 7 days for portraits, 14–21 for events, quoted for weddings.

Can I book for commercial use?

Yes — select Commercial in the request form and it’s priced into your match. The license is written into the brief so there’s no ambiguity later.

How do I choose my photographer?

You don't — that's our job. Tell us the job and we match the photographer whose specialty and track record fit it. If the match isn't right, we rematch you or refund your deposit.
