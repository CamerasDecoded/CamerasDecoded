Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Cameras Decoded is a learning and community platform for photographers. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what we never do with it.

What We Collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, and profile photo when you create an account or sign in with a provider such as Google.
  • Your content: photos you upload, posts and comments you publish, Arena entries and votes, galleries you build, and notes clients leave on gallery photos.
  • Your activity: lessons completed, XP, streaks, badges, game scores, and purchases of digital products and downloads you claim.
  • Payment records: when you buy Pro or a product, our payment processor (Stripe) handles your card. We never see or store your full card number. We keep a record of what you bought and when.
  • Newsletter information: if you subscribe on Substack or via a form, we receive your email address for delivery.

How We Use It

  • To run the platform: your account, your progress, your galleries, and the community features you use.
  • To show your public activity: posts, Arena entries, and milestones you generate appear in The Signal feed and on leaderboards, with your display name and profile photo.
  • To process payments, deliver downloads, and provide support.
  • To send email you asked for (newsletters, receipts, account notices). You can unsubscribe from marketing email at any time.

What We Never Do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not share your data with advertisers.
  • We do not make your private account information public. What appears in the feed is what the platform is designed to show: your display name, profile photo, and your public activity.

Who Processes Data For Us

  • Google Firebase: accounts, database, and platform data.
  • Stripe: payments and subscriptions.
  • Cloudinary: hosting for images you upload.
  • Substack: newsletter delivery.

Each provider processes data under its own privacy policy, only to provide its service to us.

Your Content & Visibility

  • Feed posts and comments are public to anyone who visits The Signal.
  • Client galleries are visible to anyone with the gallery link. Comments clients leave on gallery photos are visible to you and to anyone viewing that gallery.
  • Free downloads require an account so we can deliver them to your library and prevent abuse.

Your Rights

  • You can view and update your profile information in your account settings.
  • You can ask us what data we hold about you, ask for corrections, or ask us to delete your account and data: email signal@camerasdecoded.com.
  • Deleting your account removes your profile and private data. Public posts you made may remain visible without your name attached, so the community record stays intact.

Storage & Cookies

The site stores progress, preferences, and sign-in state on your device (local storage and cookies) so the platform works between visits. We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

Children

Cameras Decoded is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will update this page and the date above.
